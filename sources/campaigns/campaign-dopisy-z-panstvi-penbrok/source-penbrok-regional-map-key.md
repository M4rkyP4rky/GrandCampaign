- Descriptive labels used for unnamed records are archive reference labels only, not fictional proper names.
- `Mylsen's`, `Clairmond's`, and `Lymsey's` establish named places, not separate namesake beings.
- Marker `47` and marker `48` are distinct physical locations sharing the name `Houghbury`; existing played evidence safely identifies the pre-existing Houghbury record as the town at marker `48`.
