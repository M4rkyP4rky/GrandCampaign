# Návrat k poničenému hradu a volba záchrany Blanche

## Summary

Returning toward Penbrokton, the group learns the assault losses and uses Kirsten’s clue about the old mill to prioritize Blanche’s rescue, sending instructions to the castle from a distance. The notes put the mill clue in the crypt sequence before the return, while the final letter narrates being informed at the aftermath; this does not prove the clue was first learned on return.
