# Odjezd Kirsten a Nikolasovo ovládnutí hradu

## Summary

The retrospective quest log describes Kirsten trying for a time to break the curse after the lord’s death and then leaving in a black carriage. The notes explicitly place her disappearance after the curse began to manifest and Nikolas’s arrival afterward. The party learns and investigates these earlier developments later; they are not enacted anew by the report.
