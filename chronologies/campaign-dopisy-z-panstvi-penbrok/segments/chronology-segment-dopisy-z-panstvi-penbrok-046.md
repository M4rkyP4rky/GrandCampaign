# Spojený boj a porážka Ruthwena

## Summary

The detailed notes continue the crypt hunger and feeding into the joint battle above, garlic, mist and rats, Kirsten’s dangerous feeding and Nathaniel’s final blow. Yann’s later letter compresses these events and places hunger before the vampire’s arrival. Both account sequences are retained; whether they refer to distinct manifestations of hunger is unresolved. Burning and the crypt’s closure follow in the accounts, but no exact clock time is imposed on the final blow or disposal.
