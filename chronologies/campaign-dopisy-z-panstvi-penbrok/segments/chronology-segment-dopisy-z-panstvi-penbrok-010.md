# Hadí jeskyně a přijetí dvou vojáků

## Summary

The party confronts Imperial soldiers in the caves and accepts two survivors into service. Yann explicitly says it did not fight the snake. The quest log later identifies the unit as Serpents and the serving pair as Pierre and Viktor. Repeated full-moon deliveries of victims are reported earlier activity, not dates for this expedition; the later Hamswic account locates the priest’s defeat in that later episode.
