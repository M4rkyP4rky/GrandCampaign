## Summary

The Sezení 2 notes report Cecily’s earlier capture and wish at the well: she thinks she has been there a week, the notes say ten days, and place the courtyard visit four days earlier. The capture details include an authoring prompt ("detaily jsou na mně"). These are reported past and mixed character/authorial information, not ten days of the party’s current adventure. The prep has a different pre-play reference point ("Předevčírem"); it does not date the played encounter.
