# Vlci, kaple a noc na zpáteční cestě

## Summary

After the camp the group is pursued by wolves, defends a chapel and villagers, and Kirsten feeds on a dying wolf. The notes include a nighttime bat-form visitor seen by Oswyn and departure before dawn; only later, beyond the forest, does the party see a glow and suspect a village fire. This is suspicion, not directly witnessed arson.
