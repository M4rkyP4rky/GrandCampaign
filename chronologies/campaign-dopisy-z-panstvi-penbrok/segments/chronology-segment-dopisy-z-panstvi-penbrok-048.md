# Noční zteč Penbroku mimo družinu

## Summary

While the party is away the undead storm Penbrok at night. The notes place their attack after the vampire’s death and describe many undead falling at sunrise; the letter reports Barbra’s death during the assault. No common start or identical duration with the monastery battle is inferred. The party learns the assault’s outcome on return.
