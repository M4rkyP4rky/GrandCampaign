# Forgotton: přenocování a ztracená paměť

## Summary

The party questions villagers about repeated black-carriage journeys and chooses to stay at the inn. The notes then describe morning departure without memory of the night, with garlic at the saddles. The reported repeated journeys precede discovery; a missing memory is not a fabricated account of what occurred overnight.
