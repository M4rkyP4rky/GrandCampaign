# Nad kryptou: zrcadlová léčka

## Summary

Nathaniel, Oswyn, soldiers and Pírko prepare and watch the upper monastery trap while Yann guards Kirsten below. The notes say Ruthwen reaches the mirrors and turns into mist before mentioning Kirsten’s hungry turn. This source-local sequence is retained without deciding it is the same hunger onset as the final letter’s callback.
