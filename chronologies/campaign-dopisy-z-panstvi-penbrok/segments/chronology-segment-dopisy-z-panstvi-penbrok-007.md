# Audience a rozhodnutí o hadí výpravě

## Summary

Nathaniel receives villagers from Old Chalford and Penbrokton at the court and undertakes to address their problems. The letter connects that commitment to departure for the snake locality. The quest log describes property disputes resolved during and after the audience without dating the later resolutions.
