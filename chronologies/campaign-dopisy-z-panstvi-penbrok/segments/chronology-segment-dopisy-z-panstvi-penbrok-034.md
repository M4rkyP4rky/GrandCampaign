# Další výprava a vyhnání dřevorubců

## Summary

The notes say the last letter reached Blanche but no answer returned. The party prepares for the vampire expedition with Barbra and Cecily, then meets the crow-woman again and drives away the lumberjacks. The crow-woman reports Ruthwen’s tower plans for Blanche. Her information describes earlier or ongoing distant activity; it does not establish Blanche is already in the tower.
