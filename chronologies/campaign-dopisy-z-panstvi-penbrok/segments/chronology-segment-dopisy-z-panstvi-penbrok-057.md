# Epilog: Oswyn se učí alchymii u Neveah

## Summary

The notes say Oswyn remains on the estate and in time goes to the marsh witch to learn alchemy. The final letter says he has settled in the woods and is studying when Yann later talks with him. This is a continuing later state, not an exactly timed departure or evidence of simultaneity with Nathaniel’s and Yann’s transitions.
