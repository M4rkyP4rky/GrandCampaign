# Epilog: Yannova kniha, návštěvy a odchod

## Summary

The notes describe widowed Yann spending some time on the estate, leaving for Blanche with the newly completed book and proposing to Kirsten. The final letter recalls the court return and, after finishing his work, a short visit to Nathaniel and conversation with Oswyn, before his present life beside Kirsten. The accounts support completion before the later visit but not a complete itinerary or counted gap.
