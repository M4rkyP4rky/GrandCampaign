# Dřívější seslání ochrany Penbroku

## Summary

The retrospective quest log attributes the castle working to Neveah at the former lord’s request. Player notes separately report that the curse was already manifest during Kirsten’s residence, before her disappearance and Nikolas’s arrival. The shorter Sezení 2 explanation is hearsay, not complete objective history; explicit Sezení 4 GM/meta knowledge is not assigned to Yann.
