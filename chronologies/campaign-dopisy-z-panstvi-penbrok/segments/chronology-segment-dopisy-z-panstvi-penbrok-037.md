# Yann osvobozuje vězeňkyně a vede je podzemím

## Summary

Yann leaves the shed, frees imprisoned girls, meets Oswyn with Kirsten and leads the women through the underground route. Kirsten calms dogs; the girls go toward safety while Yann and Kirsten return for their companions. The note’s description of Kirsten’s changing attitude remains player testimony.
