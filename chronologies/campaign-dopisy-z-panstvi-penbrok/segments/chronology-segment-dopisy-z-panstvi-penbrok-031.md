# Les: Paní Vran a Desmondova stopa

## Summary

After morning departure the party encounters crows; Yann and his horse are diverted to the wetlands and he apologizes to the crow-woman. The party subsequently meets Desmond and obtains the Darkwood lead. The note’s player/meta criticism of Desmond is not retroactively character knowledge.
