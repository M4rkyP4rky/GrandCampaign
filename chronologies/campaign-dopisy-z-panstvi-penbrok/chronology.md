# Fiktivní chronologie Panství Penbrok

## Method and limits

This draft reconstructs the fictional chronology from Yann’s permanent correspondence, qualified by the player notes and retrospective quest log. Ten source sections represent eight letter units: Příjezd / Dobytí Penbroku form the first letter, and Upír / Dívky a dámy v nesnázích form one unfinished and resumed letter. Letters supply narrative traversal, while explicit fictional cues justify the stored temporal constraints. Letter headings’ dates and session numbers remain play metadata. Segment and manifest list order are navigation, never an independent total chronology.

Sources:

- [Correspondence](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml) — ten section/letter labels, explicit first-letter continuation and Darkwood-letter resumption.
- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — Sezení 1–10, matched fictional content, protagonist handoffs and epilogue.
- [Correspondence metadata](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok.md) — section-level play metadata and character-testimony classification.
- [Notes metadata](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — block-level epistemic handling and content-based session matches.

## Time layers and partial order

Each composition frame is separated from the time of its narrated scenes. The Willowburn letter explicitly starts from the ending tower outcome and returns to the earlier expedition. Earlier captive history, hangings, plague deaths, castle history, ruin destruction and Rumcombe battle are separate reported-past segments with no ordering implied between unrelated histories. The final letter is written at home after the adventure, book completion and later visits. The existing campaign record carries the approved seasonal envelope; this chronology does not spread that envelope to remembered history or the later home composition, and adds no numerical calendar anchor.

(current crypt versus remembered road), Hořkosladké vítězství (current home and extended retrospective).

## Branches, intervals and unresolved topology

The Darkwood routes split at the prisoners and rejoin before flight. The crypt vigil intersects the upper trap preparations; the later castle assault is a separate distant stream whose exact concurrence with the dawn vigil stays qualified. The mill has three explicitly separated entry routes with qualified parallelism and a shared departure. The staffing interval and later personal outcomes retain unspecified bounds. Splits, continuations and reunions are narrative relations; they do not themselves date their endpoints. Overlap claims describe intersection, not equal starts or ends.

## Account and identity cautions

Yann’s beliefs and reported information remain his account. The generic curse gossip, explicit GM/meta recap and Neveah’s later disclosure are different knowledge layers. GM preparation supplies relevant background and preserved alternative detail, never unsupported played encounters. The final letter’s hunger-before-arrival phrase and notes’ post-arrival hungry turn remain separate source-local accounts pending curation. Brigitte’s affiliation remains open. “Village 32” and its chapel remain source labels here; no new identity match or missing event is created. The old mill and camp use existing marker identities 66 and 33 supported by the played notes. Character-definition childhood/Astwell events are intentionally outside this correspondence reconstruction and need no mapping.
