# Old Chalford — prepared pre-play baseline

## Local report and concealed explanation

The notes prepare a longstanding local report of nighttime flashes near the tower, especially on moonlit nights, and a recent belief that devils have emerged and occupied the castle. Locals consequently avoid it. Hidden GM material instead identifies a damaged entrance portal whose disrupted pattern should create a force bridge and open access lower down. The notes do not establish exact dates or whether inhabitants know this mechanism.

Sources:

- [Rough GM notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrokton-Shelswell-GM-notes.md) — `# Old Chalford`, blocks beginning `historický vždycky se u věže` and `v poslední době prý`.

## Prepared occupants and deterrents

The supposed devils are prepared as a group of goblins seeking the wild past and ancestral wisdom of their people while trying to enter the tower. They stage glowing, ritual-costumed appearances to frighten villagers and have brought or lured winged stone guardians to the lower courtyard as a further deterrent. The notes do not establish that player characters encountered them or that every contemplated response occurred.
