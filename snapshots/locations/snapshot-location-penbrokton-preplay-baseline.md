# Penbrokton — prepared pre-play baseline

## Settlement and inhabitants

The rough notes prepare Penbrokton as a village where many men died in the war and women form most of the remaining population. Allen and David are nearing adulthood; Barnaby is an elderly former fishkeeper who still tries to command the village despite being ignored; Benedict is the disabled blacksmith; and Tristan and Michael are also named among the few male inhabitants. Elinor is the village leader. The source supplies no exact population, fictional date, surnames, or further characterization for Tristan and Michael.

## Prepared relationships and hidden state

Elinor is prepared as Barbra's mother and as the leader who negotiated tribute with the robber knights at Penbrok castle. Barbra is involved with Nikolas and hopes the relationship will make her noble; the existing player notes independently identify her as Elinor's daughter and Nikolas as the robber knight. A damaged sentence appears to present that relationship as the hidden reason the local women are left alone, but it is not clear enough to replace the explicit tribute arrangement with a more precise causal reconstruction. The source depicts Barbra's hope of nobility as unreciprocated. Joyce is prepared as knowing or believing that Nathaniel exploits Barbra. The rough notes do not define the nature or occasions of that exploitation, so no external event is reconstructed from Joyce's knowledge statement.

## Prepared motives, intentions, and plans

Barnaby wants to direct the village despite lacking support. Benedict seeks a cure promised by Neveah and has recruited Allen, David, and Joyce to obtain snake skin from a cave in the dark forest. At the time of Yann's arrival in Penbrokton, the three were still planning and had not undertaken the expedition. Joyce wants to be a fighter and would kill Nathaniel if an opportunity arose because of what she knows or believes about Barbra. Elinor opposes the tribute arrangement, distrusts men, and would prefer to solve the robber-knight problem by poison. Barbra wants the hostage at the castle removed out of jealousy and fear of losing Nikolas's attention. These are dispositions, intentions, and a proposed expedition, not records that any killing, poisoning, removal, or expedition occurred.
