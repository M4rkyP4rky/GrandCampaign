# Usedlost Shelswell — prepared pre-play baseline

## Household and visible state

The preparation presents Shelswell as a prosperous fish-producing estate in an unsafe surrounding area. Its public spaces are ostentatious while much of the property is worn or neglected. Adrian and Emma Northfeldovi live there with their recently adult son Thomas; Jerome Hobart serves as majordomo and cook, and Tobiáš as fishkeeper. The old mill is partly repaired, the pond dam is maintained, and the underground areas remain concealed.

Sources:

- [GM preparation](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/025-Usedlost-Shelswell-Campaign-version.docx) — opening description and keys 1–6.

## Hidden prepared state

The source prepares a hereditary Shelswell practice in which Jerome and Tobiáš keep a giant white worm underground and use its excretions in fish brine. Consumption is prepared to induce compliance, friendliness, and good mood while risking strong dependence; raw slime has a more immediate bodily effect. Emma inherited the secret and continues the family's practice of welcoming important or potentially dangerous guests with the fish. Human as well as animal remains are present in the worm cave, but no victim identities or fictional dates are supplied.

Sources:

- [GM preparation](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/025-Usedlost-Shelswell-Campaign-version.docx) — hidden backstory under key 6 `Hráz rybníka` and keys 8–10.

## Prepared knowledge and motivations

Emma knows the hereditary secret and wants Thomas married. Jerome and Tobiáš know and operate the hidden mechanism. Adrian is spending family wealth on mill repairs as cover for searching for the secret, while Emma, Jerome, and Tobiáš know about his search and let it continue. Thomas has returned from travel and wants to learn where the family's money comes from but has not yet discovered the answer. These are prepared knowledge and motivation states, not evidence that any intended or contingent action later occurred.
