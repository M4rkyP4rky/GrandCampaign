# Usedlost Shelswell — prepared pre-play baseline

## Household and visible state

The preparation presents Shelswell as a prosperous fish-producing estate in an unsafe surrounding area. Its public spaces are ostentatious while much of the property is worn or neglected. Adrian and Emma Northfeldovi live there with their recently adult son Thomas; Jerome Hobart serves as majordomo and cook, and Tobiáš as fishkeeper. The old mill is partly repaired, the pond dam is maintained, and the underground areas remain concealed.

## Hidden prepared state

The preparations describe a hereditary Shelswell practice in which a giant white worm was fed animal carcasses and hunting remains and its excretions were used in fish brine. The rough notes say Jerome fed it a dead soldier during the war when game was scarce, after which the fish became more desirable; the family then used meals for members of hostile sides as a means of survival. The campaign-version preparation describes consumption as inducing compliance, friendliness, and good mood while risking strong dependence, and raw slime as having a more immediate bodily effect. Human as well as animal remains are present in the worm cave, but no victim identities or exact fictional dates are supplied.

The rough notes also prepare a separate ancestral secret: the old mill never operated as a mill but concealed the construction of the pond and channel for a counterfeiting workshop that remains hidden in a pyrite mine in the hill. This additional hidden state is not invalidated by its absence from the campaign-version preparation.

## Prepared knowledge and motivations

Emma has inherited knowledge of the Shelswell practice and wants Thomas married before passing it to the next generation; the rough notes specify that she does not know the recent use of corpses. Jerome and Tobiáš know and operate the hidden fish mechanism, while Jerome is under pressure to obtain further bodies as local supply runs out. Adrian is spending family wealth on mill repairs and searching for the ancestral secret rather than attending to the fish operation. The rough notes say clues noticed during an imprecisely placed escape through the secret passage are beginning to make sense to him; this does not establish that he knows the worm mechanism or provide an event date. Thomas has returned from studies, wants to learn where the family's money comes from, and wants to assume family management, but has not yet discovered the operative secret. These are prepared knowledge, pressure, and motivation states, not evidence that any intended or contingent action later occurred.
