# Escort of refugees to Naděje

## Description and outcome

After the inland party divides, Bjorn takes responsibility for Ravel, Překvapení, and the refugees travelling toward Naděje. The journey lasts several days and brings the escorted group to the settlement.

Outcome: The escorted refugees reach Naděje.
