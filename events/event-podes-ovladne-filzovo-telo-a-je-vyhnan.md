# Podes possesses Filz's body and is expelled

## Description and outcome

Podes takes control of Filz's dead body during the Svatyně and Rudý vrch branch. The possession persists through later travel and ends only after the return to Bouřný les, when Podes is expelled; Filz and Podes remain distinct beings throughout.

Outcome: Podes is expelled from Filz's body; no duplicate Filz identity is created.
