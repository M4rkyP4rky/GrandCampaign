# Tristan's vow of vengeance against Wynne before Pomsta

## Description and outcome

In the former temple of Pomsta within Karmínová věž, a voice in the water tells the group to carry it with them and avenge. Tristan swears on iron to take vengeance on Wynne, whom he names as bringing death to Rojřin stesk in Prastarý's name. The voice repeats Wynne's name and the threatening light and fire subside. The source associates Pomsta with the exchange but does not explain a transfer mechanism or settle Pomsta's ontology.

Outcome: Tristan bears an iron vow to take vengeance on Wynne; Pomsta's request is source-associated with that vow, while any metaphysical attachment or transfer remains unstated.
