# Výprava do prokletého kláštera

## Description

Yann says the group visited an unnamed village troubled by a curse, apparitions, disappearances, and disability, then explored the monastery Hamswic and ended a demonic presence. The raw played sources do not name the village, supply a fictional date, name the monastery, or establish its identity with the later site; `Hamswic` and the equivalence are curator-supplied.
