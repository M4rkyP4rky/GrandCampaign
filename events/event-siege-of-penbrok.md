# Obležení a útok na Penbrok

## Description

According to Yann, an army of warriors who neither lived nor died approached from the south and besieged the village and fortified residence. The player notes place its siege preparations at [Ekenbury](../entities/locations/location-ekenbury.md). Yann's later letter says this force stormed Penbrok during the night, caused damage comparable to an earlier war, and killed Barbra. The nature and allegiance of the force, the damage comparison, and the casualty report remain claims in the played accounts.
