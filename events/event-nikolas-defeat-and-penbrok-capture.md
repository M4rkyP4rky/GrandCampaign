# Porážka Nikolase a obsazení Penbroku

## Description

According to Yann, Nathaniel lured Nikolas from the fortified residence, Nikolas's men were scattered in an ambush, and Nikolas fell after a short fight. Yann then reports the group's ceremonial entry into Penbrokton and occupation of the fortified residence. His labels for Nikolas as an usurper and for Nathaniel's claim as legitimate remain part of his partisan account.
