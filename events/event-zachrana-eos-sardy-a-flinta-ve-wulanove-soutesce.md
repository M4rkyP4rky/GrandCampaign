# Rescue of Eos, Sarda, and Flint in Wulanova soutěska

## Description and outcome

Myrick and Tristan enter Wulanova ztracená soutěska and extract Eos, Sarda, and Flint from the danger there. Burble is lost during this branch; Sarda is later identified as affected by Stín, but the exact onset is not established.

Outcome: Eos, Sarda, and Flint are rescued; Burble is lost, and Sarda's later state retains uncertain onset.
