# Ingrid's spirit binds itself to Myrick

## Description and outcome

At the later Bouřný les reunion, Ingrid manifests as a dead spirit and binds herself to Myrick. Ingrid remains the same being previously known as his living wife.

Outcome: Ingrid enters a continuing spirit-bound relationship with Myrick.
