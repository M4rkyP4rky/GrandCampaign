# Střet v Adrianově domě

## Description

Yann says that Nathaniel's group stopped at Adrian's prosperous house while preparing to ambush Nikolas. He interprets the household's business as empowered by dark magic and Sígref Strašlivá, describes a worm-like monstrosity, deaths among Adrian's family or servants, and the killing of the creature. The curator confirmed that Sígref is only Yann's rhetorical religious interpretation of the white worm and does not identify an objective being, alternate name, manifestation, or alliance in this incident. Yann's magical cause, moral judgment, religious framing, and reported outcomes remain preserved as his testimony.

The matching household names and fish-and-worm mechanism in the player notes and GM preparation identify the location as Usedlost Shelswell and the creature as the prepared giant white worm. This identity match does not by itself establish which named family members or servants died or who knew the operative secret. Curator-supplied clarifications establish the individual outcomes and objective knowledge distribution recorded below, while Yann's broader accusation remains separately attributed testimony.

The quest log adds a broad retrospective claim that `celá rodina` was fed to the worm and that an explosion buried it. This materially strengthens the player-side outcome account but still does not name individual victims or servants. The curator separately confirmed the individual deaths recorded below; that clarification does not convert the quest log's unspecified manner of death into an individual fact.

## Objective knowledge at Yann's arrival

At the time of Yann's arrival, Adrian was unaware of the worm-slime fish operation. Thomas was suspicious about the source of the household's prosperity but had not figured out the operation. Emma, Jerome Hobart, and Tobiáš knew the operation completely. Together with their prepared roles, this makes Emma, Jerome, and Tobiáš the knowing participants and excludes Adrian and Thomas from knowing participation at that time.

This is curator-supplied objective world state. It does not establish that Yann or any other character accurately knew this distribution. Emma's complete knowledge at arrival is later state than the rough preparation's limited knowledge; the archive does not infer when or how she learned the remaining details. `Completely` is limited to the worm-slime fish operation and does not extend to unrelated Shelswell secrets.

## Curator-supplied outcome

The curator confirmed that Lady Emma Northfeldová, Thomas, Jerome Hobart, and Tobiáš died during this confrontation. The clarification establishes their deaths as world information but does not specify the manner, sequence, or agent of any individual death, and it does not establish what any character knew about those deaths.

The player notes name Brigitte during this episode, and the curator confirmed that the player characters therefore encountered her. Her exact affiliation and fate remain unknown. The curator's probable reconstruction is that she was not killed with Adrian's family, probably escaped, and was not pursued by the player characters because they regarded her as insignificant. This is not promoted to a definite outcome.
