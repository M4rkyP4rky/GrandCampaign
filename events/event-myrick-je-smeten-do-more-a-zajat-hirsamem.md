# Myrick is swept to sea and captured by Hirsam

## Description and outcome

During the ice-crossing episode, Myrick is swept into the sea and separated from Tristan. The sequence continues into Myrick's capture by Mistr Hirsam and his people.

Outcome: Myrick becomes Hirsam's captive after being separated from Tristan.
