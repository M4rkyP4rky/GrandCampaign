# Rescue of the trapped miners in Údolí kleče

## Description and outcome

In Údolí kleče, Myrick and Tristan reach the miners stranded behind a barricade and establish the chimney route as a possible way out. The miners take that route ahead of them; Myrick and Tristan later emerge and follow their trail to the miners' outside camp. By the closing source occurrence the rescued group has food and can maintain a fire. Fanir is the miner from Údolí kleče, not Fanir z Rudoskal.

Outcome: The trapped miners leave the mine and reach an outside camp with food and fire available; the rescue is complete even though danger from undead Tenura remains nearby.

The source labels now navigate to the affliction [Nemoc](../entities/afflictions/affliction-nemoc.md) and the reusable being classification [Zlomení](../classifications/beings/being-classification-zlomeni.md). These links do not assert that either is a participant in the rescue.
