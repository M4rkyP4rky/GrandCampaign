# Infiltration of the armed camp and freeing of the women

## Description and outcome

Bjorn, Tristan, Finna, and their allies infiltrate the armed camp where women are being held. They free the captives and withdraw from the camp before the following weather crisis.

Outcome: The women held in the armed camp are freed.
