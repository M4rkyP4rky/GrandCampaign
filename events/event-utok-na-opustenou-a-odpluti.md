# Attack on Opuštěná and departure from Šedá řeka

## Description and outcome

During the evacuation crisis, the group reaches Opuštěná amid attack and disorder. Myrick is brought aboard, the passengers commit to escape by sea, and Opuštěná leaves Šedá řeka.

Outcome: The protagonists and passengers escape Šedá řeka aboard Opuštěná.
