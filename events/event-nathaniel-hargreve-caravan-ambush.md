# Přepadení karavany a Nathanielův útěk

## Description

When Nathaniel was a boy, bandits hired by his unnamed uncle attacked a caravan. Nathaniel's parents were killed in the attack. A blade struck Nathaniel from his horse and left the long facial scar he still bears; he survived, took the family sword [Zášť](../entities/objects/object-zast.md), and escaped through the forest.
