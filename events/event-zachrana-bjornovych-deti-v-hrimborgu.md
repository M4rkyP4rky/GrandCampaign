# Rescue of Bjorn's children at Hrimborg

## Description and outcome

Bjorn and Finna pursue the danger facing their children in the Hrimborg branch. The expedition recovers Mioll, Asmund, Sigarr, and Nami and brings the immediate family-rescue objective to its conclusion.

Outcome: Bjorn's children are recovered from the immediate danger.
