# Uzurpace Astwellu Nathanielovým strýcem

## Description

After the caravan ambush and Nathaniel's escape, his unnamed uncle had him declared dead and took control of [Astwell](../entities/polities/polity-astwell.md). The character sheet defines the uncle as a usurper, Nathaniel as the rightful heir, and the uncle's later attempts to have Nathaniel killed as repeated actions during this unresolved succession. Nathaniel's stated intention to return to the ancestral castle and avenge his parents remains a future intention and is not recorded as a completed event.
