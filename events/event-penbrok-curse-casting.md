# Seslání ochranného prokletí na Penbrok

## Description

The GM preparation and player quest log both describe Neveah performing a protective working at the request of Penbrok's lord after he learned that a vampire was visiting his wife. Existing records identify the couple as Edward and Kristen, while a curator resolution separately identifies the preparation's unnamed vampire as Lord Ruthwen. Those identity resolutions are not attributed to the quest log. The resulting effect produced deathlike prolonged sleep at the castle. The quest log's associated item remained open because breaking or controlling the curse was not marked closed; that workflow status does not negate the earlier casting or establish the curse's ultimate fate.

The player explicitly does not remember why Neveah altered or mishandled the working and offers a speculative motive. That uncertainty is retained and does not replace the GM preparation's hidden baseline.
