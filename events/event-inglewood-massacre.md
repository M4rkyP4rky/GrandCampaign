# Masakr u Inglewoodu

## Description

The player notes name the event `Masakr u Inglewoodu` and recount the rebellion, punitive hangings, apparitions, and a vision as a played-source account. The quest log locates the gallows at marker `72`, says the whole village was hanged for helping Lišky against Kohouti, and reports how the vision was triggered. The village itself and the executing force remain unnamed; opposition to Kohouti does not by itself establish who carried out the punishment, and the supernatural interpretation is not promoted to objective reconstruction.
