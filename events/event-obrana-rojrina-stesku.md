# Defense of Rojřin stesk

## Description and outcome

Undead attackers threaten Rojřin stesk during the night. The defenders stop them; by dawn the settlement is burying the dead and recognizes that the immediate assault has ended.

Outcome: The immediate undead attack on Rojřin stesk is repelled.
