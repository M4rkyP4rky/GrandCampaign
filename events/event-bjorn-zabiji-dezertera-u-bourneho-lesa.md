# Bjorn kills the threatening deserter near Bouřný les

## Description and outcome

Near Bouřný les, Bjorn pursues the deserter who killed Ketill and threatened the local family. With Burble's help he finds and kills the man, then disposes of the body.

Outcome: The deserter is killed and no longer threatens the family near Bouřný les.
