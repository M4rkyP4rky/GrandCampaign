# Smrt nakažené jednotky Kohoutů v Morových Blatech

## Description

The quest log reports that a plague-infected unit of Kohouti died in Morová Blata. It further attributes the poisoning of Willowburn's water to the dead soldiers' retaliation and says their spirits later found rest after the group reconciled them with the villagers. The deaths, supernatural causality, and motivation remain claims of this player-retrospective source; the later rites are represented separately.
