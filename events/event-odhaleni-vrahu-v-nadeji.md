# Exposure of the killers in Naděje

## Description and outcome

Bjorn brings bound suspects before Vesna and other people of Naděje and identifies them as responsible for the settlement's unexplained killings. He leaves their judgment to the community after exposing the threat.

Outcome: The suspected killers are exposed and delivered to Naděje for judgment.
