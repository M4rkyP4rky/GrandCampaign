# Pomsta enters an unknown man

## Description and outcome

Soon after leaving Bílá voda, blood is drawn through cracked stone into an unknown man and drives his attack. Bjorn identifies the entering force cautiously as perhaps Pomsta; Tristan pins the man with a spear and Bjorn and Finna join the ensuing fight. The record preserves both the source's Pomsta attribution and its expressed uncertainty.

Outcome: The unknown man becomes controlled by a blood-associated power identified tentatively as Pomsta, and the encounter turns into an active confrontation; these posts do not establish its final resolution.
