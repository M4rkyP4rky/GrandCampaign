# Rescue from Fanir's raider camp

## Description and outcome

Fanir z Rudoskal's raiders hold and torture captives at their marsh camp. Bjorn, Myrick, Nan, Finna, and Burble take part in the intervention that reaches the camp and frees the captives from the raiders.

Outcome: The captives are freed from Fanir z Rudoskal's raider camp.
