# Recovery of the inheritance in flooded Kazeera

## Description and outcome

Myrick and Tristan enter the already flooded Kazeera in pursuit of the sought inheritance. They recover it during the encounter involving Anunna and Sodden; the settlement's flooded condition remains a separate durable location state.

Outcome: The inheritance is recovered; Kazeera remains recorded separately as flooded.
