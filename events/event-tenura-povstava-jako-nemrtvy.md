# Tenura rises as an undead threat

## Description and outcome

After his death, Tenura rises again as an undead threat. He remains the same being, while his reanimation leaves Údolí kleče under continuing danger.

Outcome: Tenura becomes an undead threat and Údolí kleče remains endangered.
