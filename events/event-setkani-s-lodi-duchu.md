# Encounter with the ghost ship

## Description and outcome

During the voyage, the crew approaches an apparently abandoned vessel that proves to be a supernatural danger. Myrick goes overboard during the encounter and reaches safety again while the others keep the ship and its passengers clear of the threat.

Outcome: Myrick returns to safety and the voyage continues after the supernatural encounter.
