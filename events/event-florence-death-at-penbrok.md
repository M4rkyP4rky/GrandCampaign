# Smrt Florence při rituálu na Penbroku

## Description

Both the hidden GM preparation and the retrospective quest log place Florence's death during her tower research after she was invited to help with the curse. Their accounts differ in detail: the preparation includes an explosion and fire, while the quest log emphasizes that the curse overtook her during a ritual and that she died in her sleep. Both versions remain separately attributed rather than compressed into unsupported precision.
