# Nemoc

## Description

`Nemoc`, also called `Nemoc Starého světa`, is a named disease or affliction. Setting material describes a devastating wave through the Old World that killed many people and drove surviving communities to flee.

Sources:

- [RPGForum topic 17201, post 650273](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-zelezne-prisahy/rpgforum-340-normalized/topics/17201.md#post-650273) — names the disease, describes its spread and mortality, and connects it with the flight from the Old World.
- [RPGForum topic 17234, post 652345](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-zelezne-prisahy/rpgforum-340-normalized/topics/17234.md#post-652345) — attests the longer label `Nemoc Starého světa`.
- [Resolved curation item curation-zelezne-prisahy-004](../../curation/resolved.md#curation-zelezne-prisahy-004-nemoc-as-an-affliction) — curator-supplied classification decision.

## Uncertain accounts

The archive does not establish a single origin, transmission mechanism, or cure. Connections proposed among Nemoc, [Zlomení](../../classifications/beings/being-classification-zlomeni.md), elven magic, and a possible cure remain attributed theories or research aims rather than settled facts.

Sources:

- [RPGForum topic 17201, post 687955](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-zelezne-prisahy/rpgforum-340-normalized/topics/17201.md#post-687955) — presents competing explanations involving Nemoc, the Zlomení, and elven magic.
- [RPGForum topic 17231, post 688056](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-zelezne-prisahy/rpgforum-340-normalized/topics/17231.md#post-688056) — records Vislak's cure-related interpretation as a belief and research aim.
- [RPGForum topic 17234, post 652345](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-zelezne-prisahy/rpgforum-340-normalized/topics/17234.md#post-652345) — frames the proposed Nemoc–Zlomení connection as something to investigate, not an established causal fact.
