# Morová Blata

## Prepared regional identity

This record denotes genuinely unnamed mass graves interpreted at marker `06`.

`Unnamed mass graves at map marker 06` is an archive reference label only. The descriptive wording and marker number are not fictional proper names.

## Quest-log identity and history

The player quest log names marker `06` `Morová Blata` and reports that a plague-infected unit of [Kohouti](../organizations/organization-kohouti.md) died there. It attributes Willowburn's poisoned water to those dead and says the later rites brought them rest. The event and later rites retain their player-side provenance in separate event records.
