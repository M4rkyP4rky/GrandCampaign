# Willowburn

## Description

Willowburn is a village in Yann's account. He reports accusations that its inhabitants stole crops, then describes poverty, a stream, a willow grove, a miller, and rites which he believed calmed the dead and relieved the settlement's suffering. The accusations, history, and supernatural interpretation are not independently confirmed.

Sources:

- [Raw correspondence](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml) — sections `Hadí doupě` and `Hladovějící z Willowburnu`.

## Regional map reference

The curator key identifies marker `07` as Willowburn and describes a village with willows along a stream. Its `Hladovějící vesnice` characterization belongs to the undated prepared map baseline and does not overwrite the later played account or become a timeless identity claim.
