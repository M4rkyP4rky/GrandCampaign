# Síthstow

## Identity and temple dedication

Síthstow is the place-name for the site at marker `04`. The site has a desecrated temple devoted to the deity [Bezejmenná](../beings/being-bezejmenna.md). The name `Bezejmenná`, glossed by the curator as “Nameless,” identifies the deity rather than stating that Síthstow or the temple has no name. The site's prepared condition is kept in the [regional-map baseline snapshot](../../snapshots/locations/snapshot-location-penbrok-map-04-regional-map-baseline.md).

Marker `04` is a source locator, not a fictional name. The source does not establish a physical parent, faith, cause or date of desecration, or further settlement or site characteristics.
