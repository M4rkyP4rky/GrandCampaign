# Mudfeld

## Identity

The rough GM notes identify Nevaeh as coming from the place source-stated as `Blatná`. The curator corrected that place-name to `Mudfeld`. The raw wording remains unchanged, and the source does not establish Mudfeld's location type, hierarchy, extent, or relationship to the marshes where later notes place Neveah.
