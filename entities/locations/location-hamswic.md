# Hamswic

## Identity

Hamswic is the monastery explored during the earlier expedition and later used for the confrontation with Ruthwen. The raw played sources do not name the monastery or explicitly equate the two visits; both the name and the shared identity are curator-supplied information.

Provenance:

- Curator-supplied name and identity decision during curation review on 2026-08-21.
- [Curation audit](../../curation/resolved.md#curation-penbrok-005--identity-of-the-two-monasteries) — original uncertainty, source evidence, and resolution.

## Played descriptions

In the earlier account, Yann calls Hamswic a forgotten monastery near an unnamed afflicted village. The player notes head the site `Chrám` and describe numbered graves, an armored paladin, and uncertainty about who was buried there. In the later account, the group uses the same monastery to confront Ruthwen; the notes describe upper levels, catacombs, a crypt, and buried priests. These descriptions remain claims in the played accounts.

Sources:

- [Raw correspondence](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml) — sections `Prokletý klášter`, `Vrátila se snad válka?`, and `Hořkosladké vítězství`.
- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 5` and `Sezení 9`–`10`.

## Regional map reference and curator uncertainty

The curator key identifies marker `27` as Hamswic. Its rough category says `Velký vypálený klášter`, but its clarification explicitly asks `Možná spíš vypálený klášter?` and says the curator thinks the map calls for a large site and that no monastery may otherwise have been present. The archive therefore retains the map-key association while treating the burned-monastery characterization as tentative rather than as a confirmed timeless condition.

Sources:
