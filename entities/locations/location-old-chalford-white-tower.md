# Bílá věž

## Prepared regional identity

The regional-map key describes a white tower without an entrance on the hill above [Old Chalford](location-old-chalford.md) without supplying a proper name. The later player quest log calls it `Bílá věž`. The key does not establish whether or how it relates to the nearby castle ruins beyond placing both above the village. Marker `50` belongs to Old Chalford and serves only as the source locator for this unnumbered associated site.

Sources:

- [Curator map key](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Klíč%20k%20mapě%20Penbrok_Small.jpg.txt) — marker `50`, clarification describing the hill above the village.
- [Regional map](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok_Small.jpg) — marker `50` and surrounding visual context; it does not give the tower an independent marker.
- [Discord player quest log](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/discord_player_questlog.txt) — marker `50`, `Bílá věž`.

## Player-side report

The quest log records `V Bílé věži se usadili ďáblové` as an item that was not marked closed. This preserves the player-side issue without making `Bílá věž` a separate location or overwriting the GM preparation's hidden identification of the supposed devils as goblins staging appearances.
