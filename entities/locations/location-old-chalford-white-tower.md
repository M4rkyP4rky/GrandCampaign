# Unnamed white tower above Old Chalford

## Prepared regional identity

This record denotes a genuinely unnamed white tower without an entrance on the hill above [Old Chalford](location-old-chalford.md). The key does not establish whether or how it relates to the nearby castle ruins beyond placing both above the village.

`Unnamed white tower above Old Chalford` is an archive reference label only, not a fictional proper name. Marker `50` belongs to Old Chalford and serves only as the source locator for this unnumbered associated site.
