# Darkwood

## Description

Darkwood is Ruthwen's castle throughout the campaign material. It has underground areas, towers, a courtyard, servants, other inhabitants, and prisoners. The player notes' description of Darkwood as a village and its people as villagers is a curator-confirmed source error. Yann's phrase `Ruthwenovo panství` expresses Ruthwen's control of the castle and its surroundings; it does not identify a formal estate or separate polity.

Sources:

- [Raw correspondence](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml) — sections `Upír` and `Dívky a dámy v nesnázích`.
- [Player-notes source metadata](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md#curator-corrections) — curator-supplied correction of the village wording in `Sezení 6`.
- Curator-supplied clarification during curation review on 2026-08-21 — `Ruthwenovo panství` as informal control-language rather than a formal estate.

## Regional map reference

The curator key identifies marker `68` as Darkwood castle and associates it with `Lord Rutwenn`. The distinctive title and Darkwood relationship support resolution to existing [Lord Ruthwen](../beings/person-lord-ruthwen.md); the key spelling is retained as a source variant. This adds prepared regional provenance without creating a Darkwood village, polity, or duplicate lord.
