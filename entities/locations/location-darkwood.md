# Darkwood

## Description

Darkwood is Ruthwen's castle throughout the campaign material. It has underground areas, towers, a courtyard, servants, other inhabitants, and prisoners. The player notes' description of Darkwood as a village and its people as villagers is a curator-confirmed source error. Yann's phrase `Ruthwenovo panství` expresses Ruthwen's control of the castle and its surroundings; it does not identify a formal estate or separate polity.
