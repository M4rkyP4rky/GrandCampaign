## Description

Rojřin Stesk is an old settlement founded by refugees from the [Starý svět](location-stary-svet.md). Its priests believe their rituals contain [Rojra](../beings/being-rojra.md)'s curse and interpret recurring undead as connected to that curse; these explanations remain attributed beliefs.
