# Forgotton

## Played accounts

The player notes call the village `Forgotton` and later inflect the variant `Forgotten`. They place it west of Rumcombe and report inquiries there about the black coach, a suspicious inn, and missing memories. Yann's correspondence instead says the search continued through a village whose name he had forgotten. These accounts are compatible with the curator-supplied hidden state but remain the characters' and player's evidence.

Sources:

- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 6`, the `Forgotton` and `Forgottenu` passages.
- [Raw correspondence](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml) — section `Paní vran`, phrase describing a village whose name Yann had forgotten.

## Curator-supplied hidden state

Forgotton is a village protected by a supernatural effect: anyone who passes through it subsequently forgets the village. `Forgotton` is an established name rather than a placeholder or joke label. This world-level information is not automatically assigned to the player characters' knowledge.
