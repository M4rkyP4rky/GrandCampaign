# Katrin z Penbroktonu

## Prepared identity

The rough notes prepare Catherin as an aspiring witch who cares for Benedict. The player notes place Katrin in Penbrokton, likewise caring for Benedikt, and describe her magic as intuitive and untaught. This distinctive role and relationship support one being despite the spelling difference.

Sources:

- [Rough GM notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrokton-Shelswell-GM-notes.md) — `Penbrokton`, block `Významné ženské`.
- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 3`, `Penbrokton`.

## Played account

The player notes say Yann consulted Katrin about the Penbrok curse and record her view that the sleeping squire Lorenz should return to the grave. A later event account attributes to her an explanation concerning Willowburn. These are played-note reports and do not automatically establish objective supernatural truth.

Sources:

- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 3` and the Willowburn material in `Sezení 4`.
- [Willowburn event](../../events/event-willowburn-rites.md) — provenance-preserving played account.

## Quest-log staffing description

The quest log places Cathrin under the ongoing staff-hiring block and describes her as an aspiring witch with insight into people and sensitivity to the supernatural. It says books and instruction in reading could make her more capable. The distinctive role matches Katrin/Catherin in the other sources; the conditional development is retained as potential, not as an accomplished education or hire.
