# Lady Emma Northfeldová

## Prepared identity and relationships

The GM preparation identifies Emma as born into the Shelswell family, as Sir Adrian Northfeld's wife, and as Thomas's mother. It calls her the last of the Shelswell line and presents her as the inheritor of its secret. The player notes independently name Emma as Adrian's wife and Thomas as his son in the Shelswell household.

Sources:

- [GM preparation](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/025-Usedlost-Shelswell-Campaign-version.docx) — key 2 `Obytné stavení` and hidden backstory under key 6 `Hráz rybníka`.
- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 1`, `Místa`.

## Prepared knowledge and motivation

Emma is prepared as knowing the source of the estate's wealth, continuing the inherited guest-and-fish practice, and wanting to arrange Thomas's marriage. She leaves the practical handling of the fish and worm to Jerome and Tobiáš. These are hidden pre-play claims about her knowledge and motivation; the sources do not establish her fate after the confrontation at Shelswell.
