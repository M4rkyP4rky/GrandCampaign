# Yann du Bois

## Description

Yann du Bois is the in-world author and narrator of the correspondence. He presents himself as Blanche Renardová's faithful servant and as a participant in Nathaniel Hargreve's company; this self-presentation and his accounts of his conduct remain character testimony. The archive does not identify him with the curator-supplied real-world author.

## Relationships reported by Yann

Yann reports an unexpected betrothal and later marriage to Barbra, Barbra's death during the attack on Penbrok, and finally that he is continuing his life beside Kristen. These are Yann's claims; the source supplies no exact fictional dates for the changes.

Sources:

- [Raw correspondence](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml) — sections `Hladovějící z Willowburnu`, `Prokletý klášter`, and `Hořkosladké vítězství`.

## Knowledge of the Penbrok curse

During `Sezení 2`, Yann learned as gossip that a wizard had botched a protection against uninvited entrants, causing the castle's deathlike long sleep. The notes themselves label this account `ne úplně pravdivá pověst`; it is therefore Yann's hearsay rather than objective knowledge. During `Sezení 5`, Neveah told Yann about Kirsten's involvement with a vampire. That later disclosure does not by itself give Yann every hidden detail or identify the vampire.
