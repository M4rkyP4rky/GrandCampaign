## Relationships and enduring roles

Ingrid was [Myrick](being-myrick-lysb-rer.md)'s wife. She died in a storm, and her [spirit later bound itself to Myrick](../../events/event-ingridin-duch-se-vaze-na-myricka.md). The sources do not explicitly establish that Ingrid was [Eos](being-eos.md)'s mother.
