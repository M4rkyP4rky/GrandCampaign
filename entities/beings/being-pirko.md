# Pírko

## Played-note identity

The player notes identify Pírko as a falcon accompanying the group. They mention Pírko during the Darkwood escape and describe the falcon flying watch outside while the group prepared for Ruthwen at the monastery.

Sources:

- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 8` and `Sezení 9`.
