# Oswyn Waldegrave

## Description

Oswyn Waldegrave is a recurring member of Nathaniel's group in Yann's account. Yann credits him with reconnaissance, planning, fighting, and other successes. The first section initially writes his surname as `Waldergrave`; the curator identified that occurrence as an error and confirmed `Waldegrave` as the correct surname. The erroneous spelling remains preserved in the raw source and as an attested alternate form in this record.
