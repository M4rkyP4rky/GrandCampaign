# Elinor z Penbroktonu

## Prepared role, relationships, and intentions

Elinor is prepared as Penbrokton's leader and Barbra's mother. She negotiated tribute with the robber knights at Penbrok castle to protect the village, opposes the arrangement, distrusts men, and would prefer to solve the problem by poisoning the robber knight. The poison is a desired method, not an event or attempt.

- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 2` and `Sezení 4`, for Elionor's tribute agreement and Barbra as her daughter.

## Played interaction

The player notes say Yann promised Elionor that he would raise the village's problems with Nathaniel. They later say the tribute problem resolved with Nikolas's defeat and that Yann obtained study material from her in exchange for taking responsibility for Barbra. These are played-note claims without exact fictional dates.
