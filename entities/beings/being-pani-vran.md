# Paní Vran

## Source accounts

The played sources describe a bird-like or crow-associated woman encountered in the forest during the search for Kristen. The player notes say she denied serving either the vampire or Sluneční panna, opposed Ruthwen's incursions and tree-felling, and could disperse into crows. These are source and character accounts, not the basis for inferring additional powers or relationships.

- [Raw correspondence](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml) — sections `Paní vran` and `Upír`.
- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 6` and `Sezení 7`.

## Curator-supplied characterization

The curator identifies Paní Vran as a supernatural entity and local minor deity. She rapidly gained power during the war because crows had abundant dead to feed upon. This world-level characterization is curator-supplied and is not retroactively assigned to Yann's or the other characters' knowledge.
