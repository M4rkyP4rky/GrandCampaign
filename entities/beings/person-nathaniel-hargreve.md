# Nathaniel Hargreve

## Description

Nathaniel Hargreve is a central member of the group described by Yann. Yann says Nathaniel was selected to administer the Penbrok estate and repeatedly portrays his decisions and conduct in strongly laudatory, chivalric, and religious terms. The administrative claim and evaluations are retained as Yann's account, not promoted to neutral fact.

Sources:

- [Raw correspondence](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml) — Nathaniel's full name and proposed administrative role in `Příjezd`, plus Yann's recurring descriptions throughout the letters.

## Character definition and martial experience

Nathaniel is defined as the rightful heir of [Astwell](../polities/polity-astwell.md) and a member of the [Hargreve family](../organizations/organization-hargreve-family.md). A blade wound from the childhood caravan ambush left a long scar on his face. The `Gent d‘armes` aspect defines him as an experienced heavy-armored knight accustomed to full plate and a sallet's restricted view; he has fought, killed, and survived both victories and defeats despite retaining a youthful appearance.

Sources:

- [Character-sheet summary](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Character_sheets_Penbrok.txt) — `Dědic z Astwellu` and `Gent d‘armes`.
- [Caravan ambush](../../events/event-nathaniel-hargreve-caravan-ambush.md) — the separately represented childhood episode that caused the scar.

## Astwell backstory and intention

Nathaniel's parents were killed in a caravan ambush arranged by his unnamed uncle. Nathaniel escaped with the family sword, after which the uncle had him declared dead, repeatedly sent assassins after him, and ruled Astwell as a usurper. The character definition identifies Nathaniel as the true heir. His intention to return to the ancestral castle and avenge his parents is not treated as an event that has already occurred.

Sources:

- [Character-sheet summary](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Character_sheets_Penbrok.txt) — `Dědic z Astwellu`.
- [Caravan ambush](../../events/event-nathaniel-hargreve-caravan-ambush.md) and [Astwell usurpation](../../events/event-astwell-usurpation.md) — derived event records retaining the source's uncertain chronology.

## Zášť and family tradition

Nathaniel is associated with the inherited sword [Zášť](../objects/object-zast.md), which he took when he fled the ambush. His family preserves a mermaid legend, beliefs about the sword's supernatural properties and link to the family's fate, and a lakeshore or seashore presentation rite for each new family member. These remain Hargreve tradition and belief rather than verified supernatural facts.
