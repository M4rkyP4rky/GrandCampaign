# Nikolas

## Description

Nikolas is described by Yann as a robber knight, self-appointed lord, and administrator already controlling Penbrok when Nathaniel's group arrived. Yann reports that Nathaniel and Oswyn defeated him during an ambush. The pejorative characterization, political legitimacy claim, and reported outcome all retain Yann's provenance.
