# Ohnivec z Penbroku

## Prepared GM identification

The GM preparation identifies a fire being trapped in a Penbrok tower by Florence's unfinished ritual. It lists variable forms including flame, salamander, fiery rooster, small flaming humanoid, and fire magic. This is prepared hidden information and does not imply that the player characters knew its nature.

Sources:

- [GM preparation](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/000-Hrad-Penbrok.docx) — key 11 `Ohnivec`.

## Played interpretations and outcome

Yann's correspondence presents the being as an embodiment of the Sluneční panna, while the player notes explicitly record Yann's certainty in that interpretation. The played notes and correspondence say the group completed or altered the ritual and sent the being away. The archive retains the GM preparation's identification and Yann's religious interpretation as differently situated claims.

Sources:

- [Raw correspondence](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml) — section `Hladovějící z Willowburnu`, for Yann's interpretation and reported departure.
- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 4`, for Yann's stated certainty and the group's decision to send the being away.
