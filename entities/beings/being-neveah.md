# Neveah

## Prepared identity and promise

The rough GM notes identify Nevaeh as a witch from the place source-stated as `Blatná` who promised Benedict healing associated with snake skin. The curator corrected that place-name to [Mudfeld](../locations/location-mudfeld.md). Benedict recruited Allen, David, and Joyce to obtain the skin, but at the time of Yann's arrival in Penbrokton they were still planning and had not undertaken the expedition. The source does not establish that a cure was delivered. The distinctive witch name and her influence on Penbrokton support identity with the player notes' Neveah despite the transposed spelling.

## Played-note account

The player notes describe Neveah as very old, living in the marshes, and extending her life through sincere emotions received when she fulfills what others will not. They report that she gave Yann information about Kristen and the vampire, and later say Oswyn went to learn alchemy from her. These remain player-authored campaign claims and are not assigned exact fictional dates.

Sources:

- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 5` and `Sezení 10`.

## Quest-log retrospective

The quest log locates Neveah through marker `05` south of Egham and says she intervened in Penbrokton's affairs and wanted the shed skin from [Hadí jeskyně](../locations/location-penbrok-map-09.md). It also attributes the Penbrok curse to her at the original lord's request. The player explicitly remembers almost nothing about the dedicated Neveah block and does not remember why she altered or botched the curse, offering only speculation; those memory gaps do not replace the more specific prepared and played-note evidence.
