# Lord Ruthwen

## Description

Lord Ruthwen controls Darkwood castle and its surroundings in the campaign material. Yann's description of `Ruthwenovo panství` is not evidence of a formal estate or polity. Yann calls Ruthwen an upír and ďábel, attributes Kristen's altered condition and an undead force to him, and reports that Nathaniel's group defeated him in the monastery Hamswic. The monastery name is curator-supplied; the other descriptions, causal claims, and labels remain attributable to Yann.

Sources:

- [Raw correspondence](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml) — sections `Upír` through `Hořkosladké vítězství`.
- Curator-supplied clarification during curation review on 2026-08-21 — Ruthwen's control of the castle and surroundings and the non-formal meaning of `panství` in this context.
- [Hamswic curation resolution](../../curation/resolved.md#curation-penbrok-005--identity-of-the-two-monasteries) — curator-supplied name of the monastery where Ruthwen was defeated.

## Prepared curse backstory

The GM preparation's hidden backstory describes an unnamed vampire whom Kirsten invited to Penbrok and whom Edward sought to exclude by commissioning the protection that became the invitation-and-sleep curse. The curator identified that unnamed vampire as Lord Ruthwen. The source remains unnamed, and the identity decision does not by itself establish when any player character learned it.

Sources:

- [GM preparation](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/000-Hrad-Penbrok.docx) — hidden background under key 4 `Panoš a koně`.
- [Curation resolution](../../curation/resolved.md#curation-penbrok-009--identity-of-the-vampire-in-the-prepared-curse-backstory) — curator-supplied identity decision.
