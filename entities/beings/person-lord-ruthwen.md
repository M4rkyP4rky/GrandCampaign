# Lord Ruthwen

## Description

Lord Ruthwen is the lord of Darkwood in Yann's account. Yann calls him an upír and ďábel, attributes Kristen's altered condition and an undead force to him, and reports that Nathaniel's group defeated him in a monastery. These descriptions, causal claims, and labels remain attributable to Yann.
