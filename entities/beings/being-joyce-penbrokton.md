# Joyce z Penbroktonu

## Prepared characterization, knowledge, and intentions

Joyce is prepared as wanting to be a fighter like the boys. She knows or believes that Nathaniel exploits Barbra and would kill him if an opportunity arose. The notes do not define the alleged exploitation or establish any attempted or completed killing. Benedict recruited Joyce, Allen, and David for a snake-skin expedition. At the time of Yann's arrival in Penbrokton, they were still planning and had not undertaken it.
