# Allen z Penbroktonu

## Prepared state

Allen is a young inhabitant of Penbrokton who is nearing adulthood in the prepared settlement state. The curator confirmed that the damaged word `line` denotes Allen, making him one of the three people Benedict recruited for a snake-skin expedition alongside David and Joyce. At the time of Yann's arrival in Penbrokton, they were still planning and had not undertaken the expedition.
