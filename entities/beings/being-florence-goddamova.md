# Florence Goddamová

## Prepared backstory

The GM preparation identifies Florence as an alchemist whom Kirsten admitted to Penbrok to help break the curse. It says Florence instead used a tower for her own research, fell asleep under the curse during an experiment, and died in the resulting explosion and fire, leaving an `ohnivec` trapped in an unfinished ritual. These are hidden prepared backstory claims; the source provides no exact fictional date.

Sources:

- [GM preparation](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/000-Hrad-Penbrok.docx) — hidden background under key 4 `Panoš a koně` and key 11 `Ohnivec`.

## Player-notes reference

The player notes call Florence an alchemist and associate her with the tower experiment, but they mix character conclusions and GM/meta recap. Those notes do not replace the prepared-source provenance of the hidden backstory.
