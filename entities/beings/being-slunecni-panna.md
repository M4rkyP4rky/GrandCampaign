# Sluneční panna

## Attested divine identity

Yann repeatedly invokes Sluneční panna as his patron and describes prayer, religious symbols, protection, judgment, and an afterlife in her terms. These statements establish the named divine identity and Yann's devotion, but remain character testimony rather than an objective reconstruction of divine action or ontology.

Sources:

- [Raw correspondence](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml) — sections throughout the source, including `Příjezd`, `Dobytí Penbroku`, `Hladovějící z Willowburnu`, and `Hořkosladké vítězství`.
- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 2` through `Sezení 10`, for invocations, prayer, symbols, and Yann's interpretations.
- Curator-supplied canonical-name decision during materialization review on 2026-08-22 — `Sluneční panna` is canonical; `Sluneční paní` and `Sluneční bohyně` remain source variants.

## Penbrok fire-being interpretation

Yann identifies the trapped [Penbrok fire being](being-penbrok-fire-being.md) as an embodiment of Sluneční panna. The GM preparation instead identifies that being as an `ohnivec`. The two records remain distinct, and Yann's manifestation claim is not converted into an objective identity merge.

Sources:

- [Raw correspondence](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml) — section `Hladovějící z Willowburnu`.
- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 4`.
- [Hrad Penbrok GM preparation](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/000-Hrad-Penbrok.docx) — key 11 `Ohnivec`.

## Hortonberry chapel

The curator regional-map key identifies [Hortonberry](../locations/location-hortonberry.md) at marker `32` as a chapel of `Sluneční paní`. This establishes the prepared shrine association but does not independently establish divine action, doctrine, clergy, or the shrine's history.
