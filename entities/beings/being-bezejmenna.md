# Bezejmenná

## Attested divine identity

The player notes use `Bezejmenná` as the singular appellation of a divine being associated with a temple, prayer, and recurring symbols. The notes do not establish her nature, the meaning of the symbols, or whether individual interpretations of them are correct.

Sources:

- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 2`, Cecily block, and `Sezení 10`, `Epilog`.

## Temple at Síthstow

The curator confirmed that `Bezejmenná` in the regional map key names the deity to whom the desecrated temple at [Síthstow](../locations/location-penbrok-map-04.md) is devoted. The curator glossed the name as “Nameless.” This clarification identifies the row's referent but does not add further information about the deity's nature, worship, or the temple's desecration.
