# Barbra

## Prepared identity, relationships, and intentions

The rough GM notes and existing player notes identify Barbra as Elinor's daughter. Before Nikolas's defeat, she is prepared as secretly involved with him and hoping the relationship will make her noble, while the source depicts that hope as unreciprocated. A damaged sentence appears to present the relationship as the hidden reason Nikolas leaves the local women alone, but is not clear enough to replace the explicit tribute arrangement with a more precise causal reconstruction. Barbra wants the hostage at Penbrok removed because she is jealous and fears losing Nikolas's attention. These are hidden relationships, hopes, and an intention; they do not establish that Barbra acted against the hostage.

Sources:

- [Rough GM notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrokton-Shelswell-GM-notes.md) — `Penbrokton`, Barbra block.
- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 2` and `Sezení 4`, for Barbra as Elionor's daughter and her prior visits to Nikolas.

## Played account

Yann reports becoming engaged to Barbra during the Willowburn episode and later marrying her. The player notes continue Elinor's daughter Barbra through that marriage, supporting one identity without establishing that Willowburn was her origin or residence. Yann subsequently reports that she died during the attack on Penbrok. The sources provide neither Barbra's own perspective nor exact fictional dates for these changes.
