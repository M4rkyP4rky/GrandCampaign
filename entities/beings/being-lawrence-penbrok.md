# Lawrence

## Prepared identity and state

The GM preparation identifies Lawrence as Nicholas's squire, presents him as naively loyal to Nicholas, and says he is affected by Penbrok's sleep curse while tending the horses and fetching water for the bandits. His loyalty and the source's encounter procedures are prepared state, not a played outcome.

Sources:

- [Hrad Penbrok GM preparation](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/000-Hrad-Penbrok.docx) — key 4 `Panoš a koně`.

## Played-note name form

The player notes record Katrin's claim that a sleeping squire called `Lorenz` should return to the grave. The shared role, sleep condition, and Penbrok context support treating `Lorenz` as a source variant of Lawrence; Katrin's claim remains attributed to the notes.

Sources:

- [Player notes](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md) — `Sezení 3`, Penbrokton block, phrase `spící panoš Lorenz`.
- Curator-supplied canonical-name decision during materialization review on 2026-08-22 — `Lawrence` is canonical and `Lorenz` is retained as a source variant.
