# Roger Lowe

## Prepared identity and role

Roger Lowe is listed in the Old Chalford preparation as a flamboyantly dressed man with a red cap and feather who is paid to warn and inform. The player notes' Roger, identified in the same early campaign context as Nikolas's informant, is treated as the same being on the distinctive role match.
