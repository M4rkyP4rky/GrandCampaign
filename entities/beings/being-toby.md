# Toby

## Played-note account

The player notes identify Toby as the son of an unnamed woman in an afflicted village. They say he had disappeared in the forest, was found chained, and was returned home; the notes also relate him to the village's pattern of disappearances and journeys toward the temple and cave. These details remain a player-authored campaign account.
