# Benedict z Penbroktonu

## Prepared identity and plan

Benedict is Penbrokton's disabled blacksmith and is cared for by Katrin. Neveah has promised him healing associated with snake skin, and he recruited Allen, David, and Joyce to undertake a snake-skin expedition. At the time of Yann's arrival in Penbrokton, the group was still planning and had not undertaken it, so no expedition event is materialized.
