# Benedict z Penbroktonu

## Prepared identity and plan

Benedict is Penbrokton's disabled blacksmith and is cared for by Katrin. Neveah has promised him healing associated with snake skin, and the rough notes say he tried to persuade young villagers to undertake a snake-skin expedition. David and Joyce are clear participants in the proposal; the damaged wording may also name Allen. The notes do not clearly say the expedition occurred, so it is retained as a plan rather than an event.
