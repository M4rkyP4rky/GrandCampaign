# Sir Desmond

## Played accounts

The played sources identify Sir Desmond as a monster hunter encountered during the search for Kristen. Yann credits his help in finding a lead, while the player notes preserve more critical characterization and an explicit knowledge limitation concerning Oswyn. Neither perspective is promoted into an objective personality assessment.
