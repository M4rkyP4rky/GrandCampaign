# Barnaby z Penbroktonu

## Prepared identity and disposition

Barnaby is an elderly former fishkeeper in Penbrokton. He wants to command the village and presents himself that way when the group arrives, although the preparation says the inhabitants no longer listen to him. The player notes' distinctive local `Dědek Barneby` is treated as the same being.
