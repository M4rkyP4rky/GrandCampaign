# Yann du Bois

## Description

Yann du Bois is the in-world author and narrator of the correspondence. He presents himself as Blanche Renardová's faithful servant and as a participant in Nathaniel Hargreve's company; this self-presentation and his accounts of his conduct remain character testimony. The archive does not identify him with the curator-supplied real-world author.

## Relationships reported by Yann

Yann reports an unexpected betrothal and later marriage to Barbra, Barbra's death during the attack on Penbrok, and finally that he is continuing his life beside Kristen. These are Yann's claims; the source supplies no exact fictional dates for the changes.
