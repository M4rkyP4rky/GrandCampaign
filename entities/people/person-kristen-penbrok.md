# Kristen

## Identity and spelling

Yann describes a missing woman called Kristen who had been associated with administration of the Penbrok estate. Later sections use both `Kristen` and `Kirsten` while continuing the same disappearance, discovery at Darkwood, return, and aftermath narrative. This record provisionally treats the spellings as one person on that contextual evidence; no independent source confirms the match.

Sources:

- [Raw correspondence](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml) — the continuous narrative and both spellings from `Dobytí Penbroku` through `Hořkosladké vítězství`.

## Yann's account

Yann claims that the group found Kristen alive but altered at Darkwood, that she suffered a hunger for blood and unusual powers associated with Ruthwen, and that remaining on holy ground until dawn freed her from Ruthwen's influence after his defeat. These supernatural and causal interpretations are Yann's testimony, not a GM reconstruction.

Sources:

- [Raw correspondence](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml) — sections `Upír`, `Dívky a dámy v nesnázích`, `Vrátila se snad válka?`, and `Hořkosladké vítězství`.
