# Blanche Renardová

## Description

Blanche Renardová is the in-world addressee of Yann's correspondence. Yann describes himself as her servant, writes about the effect of events on her reputation and claims, and later reports that his group found and released her. These statements preserve Yann's perspective; the source does not independently establish Blanche's own account.
