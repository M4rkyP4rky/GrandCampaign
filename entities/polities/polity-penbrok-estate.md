# Panství Penbrok

## Description

The letters repeatedly identify a Penbrok estate or domain whose administration and control are contested or changing in Yann's narrative. `Penbrok` can be used contextually for this estate as well as for the separately recorded castle. The estate is represented as a polity rather than a physical parent: Yann reports Nikolas claiming its administration, Nathaniel being selected to administer it, the group's occupation of the fortified residence, and Kristen's earlier association with its administration. The legality and succession implied by Yann are claims within his account. The curator clarified that full control of the estate was never established during play; taking the castle therefore does not establish control of this polity.
