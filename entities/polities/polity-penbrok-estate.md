# Panství Penbrok

## Description

The letters repeatedly identify a Penbrok estate or domain whose administration and control are contested or changing in Yann's narrative. It is represented as a polity rather than a physical parent: Yann reports Nikolas claiming its administration, Nathaniel being selected to administer it, the group's occupation of the fortified residence, and Kristen's earlier association with its administration. The legality and succession implied by Yann are claims within his account.
