# Astwell

## Fief identity

The character sheet identifies Astwell as a fief (`astwellské léno`) administered by the [Hargreve family](../organizations/organization-hargreve-family.md), with Nathaniel defined as its rightful heir. It is represented as a polity rather than as physical geography. The source also mentions the family's ancestral castle but does not name it Astwell, locate it, or establish a physical hierarchy.

Source:

- [Character-sheet summary](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Character_sheets_Penbrok.txt) — Nathaniel Hargreve, `Dědic z Astwellu`.

## Contested succession

After Nathaniel's parents were killed and he escaped, his unnamed uncle had him declared dead and ruled Astwell as a usurper. The same character definition identifies Nathaniel as the rightful heir and says the uncle had repeatedly sent assassins after him. This is time-dependent backstory represented through the [Astwell usurpation event](../../events/event-astwell-usurpation.md), not a timeless property of the fief.

Source:

- [Character-sheet summary](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Character_sheets_Penbrok.txt) — Nathaniel Hargreve, `Dědic z Astwellu`.
