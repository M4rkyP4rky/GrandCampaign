# Zášť

## Identity and Nathaniel's association

Zášť is the hereditary sword of the [Hargreve family](../organizations/organization-hargreve-family.md). The character sheet says Nathaniel took the family sword when his father was killed and fled through the forest; its presentation as one of Nathaniel's defining aspects associates the blade with him without supplying later transfer or loss history.

Sources:

- [Character-sheet summary](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Character_sheets_Penbrok.txt) — Nathaniel Hargreve, `Dědic z Astwellu` and `‚Zášť‘ (čepel)`.
- [Caravan ambush](../../events/event-nathaniel-hargreve-caravan-ambush.md) — the backstory episode in which Nathaniel took the sword and escaped.

## Family legend and attributed properties

Hargreve legend says the first member of the family received Zášť from an unnamed mermaid after recovering her lost mirror of pure gold. The family tradition describes the sword as magical, unbreakable, and perpetually sharp and associates the family's fate with it. The source explicitly presents these as legend and family tradition; the archive does not promote the origin story or supernatural properties to independently verified fact.
