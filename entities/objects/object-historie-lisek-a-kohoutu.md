# Historie Lišek a Kohoutů

## Identity and naming uncertainty

Yann's final letter says he recently completed `Historie Lišek a Kohoutů` and that a substantial part concerns Panství Penbrok. The player notes describe his freshly completed book using both `Historie Lišek a Kohoutů` and the title-like form `Příběhy z Penbrokského panství`. The sources establish one completed work but do not safely determine whether the latter form is an alternate title, subtitle, internal section, or descriptive phrase.
