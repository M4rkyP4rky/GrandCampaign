# Northfeldi

## Prepared family group

The rough GM notes use `Northfeldi` collectively for the family or household at Shelswell and attribute to them a repeated wartime hosting strategy. The longer Shelswell preparation instead attributes the inherited fish tradition specifically to the Shelswell lineage through Emma. These differently scoped prepared claims are retained without silently equating the Northfeld and Shelswell families or assigning full membership and knowledge.
