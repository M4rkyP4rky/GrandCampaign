# rod Shelswellů

## Prepared lineage and tradition

The Shelswell GM preparation identifies a named lineage associated with [Usedlost Shelswell](../locations/location-shelswell-estate.md) and a multigenerational tradition involving its fish. It calls [Lady Emma Northfeldová](../beings/being-emma-northfeldova.md), born Shelswellová, the last of this lineage and says she continued the tradition after marriage. This does not establish a formal organization, title system, or the lineage's full membership.
