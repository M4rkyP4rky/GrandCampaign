# Serpents

## Player-side account

The quest log names Serpents as a military unit of the [Lví císařství](../polities/polity-lvi-cisarstvi.md) whose soldiers took refuge in [Hadí jeskyně](../locations/location-penbrok-map-09.md). It says they used human sacrifices and alchemy while trying to make equipment from snake skin, that the group dispersed them, and that two named members, [Pierre](../beings/being-pierre-serpents.md) and [Viktor](../beings/being-viktor-serpents.md), later served a punishment. The exact formal structure, strength, chain of command, and duration of the unit are not recorded.
