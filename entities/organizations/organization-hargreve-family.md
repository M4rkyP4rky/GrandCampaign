# rodina Hargrevů

## Astwell lineage

The character sheet defines the Hargreves as Nathaniel Hargreve's family and says they administer the [Astwell](../polities/polity-astwell.md) fief and an unnamed ancestral castle. Nathaniel is defined as the rightful heir. His unnamed uncle had Nathaniel declared dead and ruled Astwell as a usurper at the character-definition point; the exact wider membership and formal title system are not recorded.

## Zášť tradition

The family preserves a legend that the first Hargreve helped an unnamed mermaid recover a lost mirror of pure gold and received the sword [Zášť](../objects/object-zast.md) as a reward. Family tradition says the sword is magical, unbreakable, and always sharp, binds the family's fate, and is passed down through the lineage. The Hargreves also believe the mermaid watches over their fortune and present each new family member to her at a seashore or lakeshore for recognition and protection during journeys. These are family beliefs and practices, not independently verified supernatural facts.
