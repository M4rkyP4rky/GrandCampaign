# Open content curation items

## curation-penbrok-020 — Brigitte's affiliation during the played encounter

- Status: open
- Scope: [Brigitte](../entities/beings/being-brigitte-shelswell.md) and [Střet v Adrianově domě](../events/event-adrian-house-ordeal.md) in [Panství Penbrok](../campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok.md)
- Uncertainty: In what capacity was Brigitte encountered during the first played Shelswell episode, and what place, household, or other group—if any—was she affiliated with?
- Why it matters: The player notes establish a played encounter and call Brigitte a maid, but neither the notes nor GM preparation explicitly name her employer or origin. Treating her as a Shelswell household member could attach an unsupported relationship; leaving the played encounter unrecorded would discard curator-confirmed play evidence.
- Affected records: [Brigitte](../entities/beings/being-brigitte-shelswell.md), [Střet v Adrianově domě](../events/event-adrian-house-ordeal.md), and any future relationship or location record supported by clarification. Brigitte's permanent stable ID does not itself establish a Shelswell affiliation.
- Evidence: [Player notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md), `Sezení 1`, `Místa`, list `Brigitte děvečka (ztratil se její milý)` among material concerning Shelswell. The curator confirmed on 2026-08-22 that her presence in the player notes means the player characters encountered her. The [campaign-version Shelswell GM preparation](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/025-Usedlost-Shelswell-Campaign-version.docx) does not name her, and no other ingested source supplies an employer or origin.
- Current handling: Brigitte is recorded as a maid encountered during the played Shelswell episode, without a confirmed household membership or origin. Her exact fate is unknown. A curator-supplied probable reconstruction holds that she was not killed with Adrian's family, probably escaped, and was not pursued by the player characters because they regarded her as insignificant; none of those probable details is promoted to a definite outcome.
- Predecessor: [curation-penbrok-019](resolved.md#curation-penbrok-019--brigittes-origin-and-affiliation) was superseded after the curator withdrew its erroneous resolution.
