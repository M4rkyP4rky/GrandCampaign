- Status: open
- Scope: [Brigitte](../entities/beings/being-brigitte-shelswell.md) and [Střet v Adrianově domě](../events/event-adrian-house-ordeal.md) in [Panství Penbrok](../campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok.md)

- Affected records: [Brigitte](../entities/beings/being-brigitte-shelswell.md), [Střet v Adrianově domě](../events/event-adrian-house-ordeal.md), and any future relationship or location record supported by clarification. The curator confirmed on 2026-08-22 that her presence in the player notes means the player characters encountered her. The [campaign-version Shelswell GM preparation](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/025-Usedlost-Shelswell-Campaign-version.docx) does not name her, and no other ingested source supplies an employer or origin.

A curator-supplied probable reconstruction holds that she was not killed with Adrian's family, probably escaped, and was not pursued by the player characters because they regarded her as insignificant; none of those probable details is promoted to a definite outcome.
- Predecessor: [curation-penbrok-019](resolved.md#curation-penbrok-019--brigittes-origin-and-affiliation) was superseded after the curator withdrew its erroneous resolution.
