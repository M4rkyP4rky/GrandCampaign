# Open content curation items

## curation-penbrok-012 — Played fates of the Shelswell household

- Status: open
- Scope: [Usedlost Shelswell](../entities/locations/location-shelswell-estate.md) in [Panství Penbrok](../campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok.md)
- Uncertainty: Which of the named prepared household members other than Sir Adrian — Emma, Thomas, Jerome Hobart, Tobiáš, and the player-notes maid Brigitte — died, survived, or were otherwise affected during the played confrontation at Shelswell?
- Why it matters: The played account explicitly calls Adrian deceased and reports that unnamed family members or servants died, but assigning those outcomes to particular prepared beings would manufacture later state. Clarification could materially affect being records and any post-encounter location state.
- Affected records: [Střet v Adrianově domě](../events/event-adrian-house-ordeal.md), [Sir Adrian](../entities/beings/person-sir-adrian.md), [Lady Emma Northfeldová](../entities/beings/being-emma-northfeldova.md), [Thomas](../entities/beings/being-thomas-shelswell.md), [Jerome Hobart](../entities/beings/being-jerome-hobart.md), [Tobiáš](../entities/beings/being-tobias-shelswell.md), [Brigitte](../entities/beings/being-brigitte-shelswell.md), and any future later-state record for Shelswell.
- Evidence: [Campaign-version GM preparation](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/025-Usedlost-Shelswell-Campaign-version.docx), keys 1–6, and [rough GM notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrokton-Shelswell-GM-notes.md), `# Shellswel`, name the prepared household and present Adrian, Emma, Thomas, Jerome, and Tobiáš alive before play. [Player notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md), `Sezení 1`, additionally names Brigitte. [Correspondence](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml), section `Příjezd`, identifies Adrian as deceased and says agents of darkness among his family or servants died in the worm's jaws without naming them. The [Discord player quest log](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/discord_player_questlog.txt), marker `25`, adds the broad retrospective claim that `celá rodina` was fed to the worm and that an explosion buried the creature, but still names no individual victim and says nothing about the servants' fates.
- Current handling: Adrian's and the white worm's reported deaths remain attributed to played accounts. The quest log materially strengthens the player-side claim that all family members died, but the archive does not convert that collective wording into individually certain death states for Emma or Thomas, nor does it establish the fates of Jerome, Tobiáš, or Brigitte. The Shelswell snapshot remains strictly pre-play.

## curation-penbrok-013 — Knowledge and complicity in the Shelswell secret

- Status: open
- Scope: [Střet v Adrianově domě](../events/event-adrian-house-ordeal.md)
- Uncertainty: Which Shelswell household members knowingly participated in the addictive-fish secret at the time of the played confrontation?
- Why it matters: Promoting Yann's broad accusation to individual knowledge could conflict with the preparation's more specific knowledge distribution; treating the preparation as an automatic winner would erase a played character account.
- Affected records: [Sir Adrian](../entities/beings/person-sir-adrian.md), [Lady Emma Northfeldová](../entities/beings/being-emma-northfeldova.md), [Thomas](../entities/beings/being-thomas-shelswell.md), [Jerome Hobart](../entities/beings/being-jerome-hobart.md), [Tobiáš](../entities/beings/being-tobias-shelswell.md), and [Brigitte](../entities/beings/being-brigitte-shelswell.md).
- Evidence: [Campaign-version GM preparation](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/025-Usedlost-Shelswell-Campaign-version.docx), keys 1–6, assigns the operative secret to Emma, Jerome, and Tobiáš, presents Adrian as searching without having found it, and presents Thomas as still investigating the family's wealth. [Rough GM notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrokton-Shelswell-GM-notes.md), `# Shellswel`, identify Jerome as heading the operation, Emma as holding inherited but incomplete knowledge that excludes the recent corpse use, Adrian as focused on a distinct ancestral mill secret, and Thomas as interested but not yet informed. [Correspondence](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml), section `Příjezd`, has Yann claim that a plot involved a substantial part of Adrian's family and servants but does not identify them or distinguish knowledge from his interpretation of involvement. The [Discord player quest log](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/discord_player_questlog.txt), marker `25`, similarly attributes the old rites and mind-affecting worm slime to `místní` collectively without identifying who knew or acted.
- Current handling: The compatible preparation sources refine the prepared knowledge distribution but do not resolve who knowingly participated during the later played confrontation. Yann's and the quest log's broader collective claims remain player-side accounts in the event record; the archive does not reconcile them with the preparation or rank either automatically above the other.

## curation-penbrok-015 — Rumburk and Rumcombe identity

- Status: open
- Scope: [Thomas ze Shelswellu](../entities/beings/being-thomas-shelswell.md) and [Rumcombe](../entities/locations/location-rumcombe.md)
- Uncertainty: Does the rough-notes place-name `Rumburk`, where Thomas was sent to study, refer to the existing village Rumcombe, represent a spelling or translation variant, or denote a distinct named place?
- Why it matters: Treating the names as identical could attach Thomas's studies to the wrong location; creating a second identity without evidence could duplicate Rumcombe.
- Affected records: [Thomas ze Shelswellu](../entities/beings/being-thomas-shelswell.md) and [Rumcombe](../entities/locations/location-rumcombe.md).
- Evidence: [Rough GM notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrokton-Shelswell-GM-notes.md), `# Shellswel`, `Syn Thomas`, say Thomas was sent to study `do Rumburku`. [Player notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md), `Sezení 6`, and [correspondence](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml), `Paní vran`, identify Rumcombe as a war-damaged or burned village encountered during the search for Kristen. The curator [regional-map key](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Klíč%20k%20mapě%20Penbrok_Small.jpg.txt), marker `13`, independently retains the spelling `Rumcombe` and characterizes it as a burned village, but does not mention Thomas, Rumburk, or an equivalence. The spelling resemblance does not establish identity, and the study context does not establish distinctness.
- Current handling: The new map evidence enriches Rumcombe but does not resolve this item. Thomas's record retains the source-stated `Rumburk` without a location link. No Rumburk record is created and no link to Rumcombe is asserted pending curator evidence.

- Current handling: No separate Sígref record is created, and `Sígref Strašlivá` is not added as a confirmed alternate name of the white worm. The possible identity or manifestation relationship remains unresolved.

## curation-penbrok-018 — Identity category of æsové

- Status: open
- Scope: [Panství Penbrok regional map key](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-penbrok-regional-map-key.md), markers `26` and `50`
- Uncertainty: What persistent kind of identity, if any, does the expression `z časů æsů` denote: a named people or culture, a polity, an organization, a species or class of beings, an Age, or another historical referent?
- Why it matters: Treating the expression as a polity or organization would manufacture unsupported structure, while ignoring a confirmed proper-named people would leave the named-things invariant incomplete.
- Affected records: [marker-26 aqueduct ruins](../entities/locations/location-penbrok-map-26.md), [castle ruins above Old Chalford](../entities/locations/location-old-chalford-castle-ruins.md), and the [map-key source record](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-penbrok-regional-map-key.md).

- Current handling: The exact source expression remains in both affected location records and source metadata. No polity, organization, being, age, or duplicate identity is asserted until the referent and appropriate persistent type can be established.
