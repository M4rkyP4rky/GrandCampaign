# Open content curation items

## curation-penbrok-012 — Played fates of the Shelswell household

- Status: open
- Scope: [Usedlost Shelswell](../entities/locations/location-shelswell-estate.md) in [Panství Penbrok](../campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok.md)
- Uncertainty: Which of the named prepared household members other than Sir Adrian — Emma, Thomas, Jerome Hobart, Tobiáš, and the player-notes maid Brigitte — died, survived, or were otherwise affected during the played confrontation at Shelswell?
- Why it matters: The played account explicitly calls Adrian deceased and reports that unnamed family members or servants died, but assigning those outcomes to particular prepared beings would manufacture later state. Clarification could materially affect being records and any post-encounter location state.
- Affected records: [Střet v Adrianově domě](../events/event-adrian-house-ordeal.md), [Sir Adrian](../entities/beings/person-sir-adrian.md), [Lady Emma Northfeldová](../entities/beings/being-emma-northfeldova.md), and any future later-state record for Shelswell.
- Evidence: [GM preparation](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/025-Usedlost-Shelswell-Campaign-version.docx), keys 1–6, names the prepared household and presents Adrian, Emma, Thomas, Jerome, and Tobiáš alive before play. [Player notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md), `Sezení 1`, additionally names Brigitte. [Correspondence](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml), section `Příjezd`, identifies Adrian as deceased and says agents of darkness among his family or servants died in the worm's jaws without naming them.
- Current handling: Adrian's and the white worm's reported deaths remain attributed to Yann. No death or survival state is assigned to Emma, Thomas, Jerome, Tobiáš, or Brigitte, and the Shelswell snapshot remains strictly pre-play.

## curation-penbrok-013 — Knowledge and complicity in the Shelswell secret

- Status: open
- Scope: [Střet v Adrianově domě](../events/event-adrian-house-ordeal.md)
- Uncertainty: Which Shelswell household members knowingly participated in the addictive-fish secret at the time of the played confrontation?
- Why it matters: Promoting Yann's broad accusation to individual knowledge could conflict with the preparation's more specific knowledge distribution; treating the preparation as an automatic winner would erase a played character account.
- Affected records: [Sir Adrian](../entities/beings/person-sir-adrian.md), [Lady Emma Northfeldová](../entities/beings/being-emma-northfeldova.md), and any future being records for Thomas, Jerome Hobart, Tobiáš, or Brigitte.
- Evidence: [GM preparation](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/025-Usedlost-Shelswell-Campaign-version.docx), keys 1–6, assigns the operative secret to Emma, Jerome, and Tobiáš, presents Adrian as searching without having found it, and presents Thomas as still investigating the family's wealth. [Correspondence](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml), section `Příjezd`, has Yann claim that a plot involved a substantial part of Adrian's family and servants but does not identify them or distinguish knowledge from his interpretation of involvement.
- Current handling: Prepared knowledge states remain sourced to the GM preparation. Yann's broader claim remains character testimony in the event record; the archive does not reconcile the accounts or rank one automatically above the other.
