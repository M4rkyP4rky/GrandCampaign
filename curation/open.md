## curation-penbrok-012 — Played fates of the Shelswell household

- Status: open
- Scope: [Usedlost Shelswell](../entities/locations/location-shelswell-estate.md) in [Panství Penbrok](../campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok.md)
- Uncertainty: Which of the named prepared household members other than Sir Adrian — Emma, Thomas, Jerome Hobart, Tobiáš, and the player-notes maid Brigitte — died, survived, or were otherwise affected during the played confrontation at Shelswell?
- Why it matters: The played account explicitly calls Adrian deceased and reports that unnamed family members or servants died, but assigning those outcomes to particular prepared beings would manufacture later state. Clarification could materially affect being records and any post-encounter location state.
- Affected records: [Střet v Adrianově domě](../events/event-adrian-house-ordeal.md), [Sir Adrian](../entities/beings/person-sir-adrian.md), [Lady Emma Northfeldová](../entities/beings/being-emma-northfeldova.md), [Thomas](../entities/beings/being-thomas-shelswell.md), [Jerome Hobart](../entities/beings/being-jerome-hobart.md), [Tobiáš](../entities/beings/being-tobias-shelswell.md), [Brigitte](../entities/beings/being-brigitte-shelswell.md), and any future later-state record for Shelswell.
- Evidence: [Campaign-version GM preparation](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/025-Usedlost-Shelswell-Campaign-version.docx), keys 1–6, and [rough GM notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrokton-Shelswell-GM-notes.md), `# Shellswel`, name the prepared household and present Adrian, Emma, Thomas, Jerome, and Tobiáš alive before play. [Player notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md), `Sezení 1`, additionally names Brigitte. [Correspondence](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml), section `Příjezd`, identifies Adrian as deceased and says agents of darkness among his family or servants died in the worm's jaws without naming them.
- Current handling: Adrian's and the white worm's reported deaths remain attributed to Yann. No death or survival state is assigned to Emma, Thomas, Jerome, Tobiáš, or Brigitte, and the Shelswell snapshot remains strictly pre-play.

## curation-penbrok-013 — Knowledge and complicity in the Shelswell secret

- Status: open
- Scope: [Střet v Adrianově domě](../events/event-adrian-house-ordeal.md)
- Uncertainty: Which Shelswell household members knowingly participated in the addictive-fish secret at the time of the played confrontation?
- Why it matters: Promoting Yann's broad accusation to individual knowledge could conflict with the preparation's more specific knowledge distribution; treating the preparation as an automatic winner would erase a played character account.
- Affected records: [Sir Adrian](../entities/beings/person-sir-adrian.md), [Lady Emma Northfeldová](../entities/beings/being-emma-northfeldova.md), [Thomas](../entities/beings/being-thomas-shelswell.md), [Jerome Hobart](../entities/beings/being-jerome-hobart.md), [Tobiáš](../entities/beings/being-tobias-shelswell.md), and [Brigitte](../entities/beings/being-brigitte-shelswell.md).
- Evidence: [Campaign-version GM preparation](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/025-Usedlost-Shelswell-Campaign-version.docx), keys 1–6, assigns the operative secret to Emma, Jerome, and Tobiáš, presents Adrian as searching without having found it, and presents Thomas as still investigating the family's wealth. [Rough GM notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrokton-Shelswell-GM-notes.md), `# Shellswel`, identify Jerome as heading the operation, Emma as holding inherited but incomplete knowledge that excludes the recent corpse use, Adrian as focused on a distinct ancestral mill secret, and Thomas as interested but not yet informed. [Correspondence](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml), section `Příjezd`, has Yann claim that a plot involved a substantial part of Adrian's family and servants but does not identify them or distinguish knowledge from his interpretation of involvement.
- Current handling: The compatible preparation sources refine the prepared knowledge distribution but do not resolve who knowingly participated during the later played confrontation. Yann's broader claim remains character testimony in the event record; the archive does not reconcile the accounts or rank one automatically above the other.

## curation-penbrok-015 — Rumburk and Rumcombe identity

- Status: open
- Scope: [Thomas ze Shelswellu](../entities/beings/being-thomas-shelswell.md) and [Rumcombe](../entities/locations/location-rumcombe.md)
- Uncertainty: Does the rough-notes place-name `Rumburk`, where Thomas was sent to study, refer to the existing village Rumcombe, represent a spelling or translation variant, or denote a distinct named place?
- Why it matters: Treating the names as identical could attach Thomas's studies to the wrong location; creating a second identity without evidence could duplicate Rumcombe.
- Affected records: [Thomas ze Shelswellu](../entities/beings/being-thomas-shelswell.md) and [Rumcombe](../entities/locations/location-rumcombe.md).
- Evidence: [Rough GM notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrokton-Shelswell-GM-notes.md), `# Shellswel`, `Syn Thomas`, say Thomas was sent to study `do Rumburku`. [Player notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md), `Sezení 6`, and [correspondence](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml), `Paní vran`, identify Rumcombe as a war-damaged or burned village encountered during the search for Kristen. The spelling resemblance does not establish identity, and the study context does not establish distinctness.
- Current handling: Thomas's record retains the source-stated `Rumburk` without a location link. No Rumburk record is created and no link to Rumcombe is asserted pending curator evidence.

## curation-penbrok-016 — Sígref Strašlivá and the Shelswell white worm

- Status: open
- Scope: [Obří bílý červ ze Shelswellu](../entities/beings/being-shelswell-white-worm.md) and [Střet v Adrianově domě](../events/event-adrian-house-ordeal.md)
- Uncertainty: Does `Sígref Strašlivá` name the Shelswell white worm in Yann's religious interpretation, a distinct divine being manifested or incarnated through the worm, or another identity relationship?
- Why it matters: Treating the names as equivalent could erase a distinct deity; creating a separate being could duplicate the worm when the phrase is only Yann's alternate identification.
- Affected records: [Obří bílý červ ze Shelswellu](../entities/beings/being-shelswell-white-worm.md), [Sir Adrian](../entities/beings/person-sir-adrian.md), and [Střet v Adrianově domě](../events/event-adrian-house-ordeal.md).
- Evidence: [Correspondence](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml), `Příjezd`, says Adrian's family was allied with `Sígref Strašlivá`, incarnated in a worm-like monstrosity. [Campaign-version GM preparation](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/025-Usedlost-Shelswell-Campaign-version.docx), key 10 and the hidden backstory under key 6, identifies a giant white worm and its fish-related mechanism without naming Sígref. The existing being record preserves the strong match between the prepared worm and the played creature while treating Sígref as Yann's religious interpretation.
- Current handling: No separate Sígref record is created, and `Sígref Strašlivá` is not added as a confirmed alternate name of the white worm. The possible identity or manifestation relationship remains unresolved.
