## curation-penbrok-009 — Identity of the vampire in the prepared curse backstory

- Status: resolved
- Scope: [Panství Penbrok](../campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok.md)
- Original uncertainty: Is the unnamed vampire whom Kirsten invited to Penbrok in the GM preparation the same being as [Lord Ruthwen](../entities/beings/person-lord-ruthwen.md)?
- Why it matters: A confirmed match would establish Ruthwen's earlier relationship with Kirsten and his role in the origin of the fortress curse; a wrong match would attach hidden backstory to the wrong being.
- Earlier evidence: [GM preparation](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/000-Hrad-Penbrok.docx), hidden background under key 4 `Panoš a koně`, describes an unnamed vampire visiting Kirsten. Played sources later identify Ruthwen as a vampire connected to Kirsten's disappearance and condition, but the GM preparation does not name him in the earlier backstory.
- Earlier handling: The prepared vampire remained unnamed and was not linked to Ruthwen in derived backstory or snapshot records.
- Resolution: The unnamed vampire in the prepared curse backstory is Lord Ruthwen.
- Basis: Curator-supplied identity decision during curation review.
- Affected records updated: [GM-preparation source metadata](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-hrad-penbrok-gm-preparation.md), [Kristen](../entities/beings/person-kristen-penbrok.md), [Edward z Penbroku](../entities/beings/being-edward-of-penbrok.md), [Lord Ruthwen](../entities/beings/person-lord-ruthwen.md), and the [prepared Penbrok snapshot](../snapshots/locations/snapshot-location-penbrok-preplay-baseline.md).

## curation-penbrok-011 — Yann's knowledge of the generic curse explanation in Sezení 2

- Status: resolved
- Scope: [Penbrok player notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md)
- Replaces: [curation-penbrok-008](#curation-penbrok-008--character-knowledge-of-the-curse-explanation)
- Original uncertainty: Did Yann know during `Sezení 2` that the long-sleep curse resulted from a botched protection against uninvited entrants, or is the block `Prokletí - ne úplně pravdivá pověst` player/GM-level or retrospective information?
- Why it matters: The hidden GM baseline was established, and Yann's later learning about Kirsten and a vampire was dated to `Sezení 5`, but attributing the earlier generic explanation to Yann without clarification would have given him knowledge the notes did not clearly assign.

- Earlier handling: The block remained player/meta-ambiguous and was not attributed to Yann, while the GM preparation independently supported the hidden baseline.
- Resolution: Yann learned this version of the explanation during `Sezení 2` as gossip. It is character hearsay and retains the notes' qualification that the account is not entirely true; it is not promoted to objective GM truth.

## curation-penbrok-004 — Extent and referents of Darkwood

- Status: resolved
- Scope: [Panství Penbrok](../campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok.md)
- Original uncertainty: Does `Darkwood` name a village, Ruthwen's wider domain, his principal castle or residence, or more than one of these?
- Why it matters: A confirmed distinction could require separate settlement, castle, and polity records and more precise event locations.
- Earlier evidence: [Dopisy z panství Penbrok](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml), sections `Upír` and `Dívky a dámy v nesnázích`, calls Darkwood a `panství` while describing a residence with underground areas and towers. [Player notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md), `Sezení 6`, appears to describe `Darkwood (68)` as a southern village whose inhabitants are affected by their lord; `Sezení 7` then uses `Darkwood` for the journey's destination and under that heading describes a `hrad`, towers, a courtyard, and servants, while `Sezení 8` describes an escape from the castle.
- Earlier handling: One location record preserved the name without asserting its exact extent or physical hierarchy.
- Resolution: Darkwood is the castle throughout. The `Sezení 6` references to a village and villagers are source errors; they mean the castle and its inhabitants. `Ruthwenovo panství` is an informal expression for Ruthwen's control of the castle and its surroundings, not a formal estate or separate polity.
- Basis: Curator-supplied source correction and referent clarification during curation review.
- Affected records updated: [Darkwood](../entities/locations/location-darkwood.md), [Lord Ruthwen](../entities/beings/person-lord-ruthwen.md), [Útěk z Darkwoodu](../events/event-darkwood-escape.md), [Pátrání po Kristen](../events/event-search-for-kristen.md), and [player-notes source metadata](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md). No village or Darkwood polity record was created.
- Resolution date: 2026-08-21 (real-world archive/curation date).

## curation-penbrok-003 — Referents of Penbrok

- Status: resolved
- Scope: [Panství Penbrok](../campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok.md)
- Original uncertainty: Which uses of `Penbrok` denote the fortified residence, the political estate, or a broader vicinity, and are any further location identities required?
- Why it matters: Conflating a physical location with a political domain would produce incorrect geography and time-dependent control data.
- Earlier evidence: [Dopisy z panství Penbrok](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml), including sections `Příjezd`, `Dobytí Penbroku`, `Hladovějící z Willowburnu`, `Vrátila se snad válka?`, and `Hořkosladké vítězství`, uses `Penbrok` alongside `panství`, `tvrz`, `pevnost`, `hrad`, and `Penbrokton` without consistently fixing the referent. [Player notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md), especially `Sezení 1`, `Sezení 4`, and `Sezení 10`, add `hrad`, `Penbrok`, `Penbrokton`, and `panství` usages but still do not define all referents. [GM preparation](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/000-Hrad-Penbrok.docx) explicitly uses `Hrad Penbrok` for the mapped physical fortress.
- Earlier handling: The fortified residence and political estate had separate records; Penbrokton remained a separate settlement, and ambiguous passages were not forced into a physical hierarchy.
- Resolution: `Penbrokton` always denotes the village adjacent to the castle. `Penbrok` can denote either the castle (`Hrad Penbrok`) or the estate (`Panství Penbrok`) according to context. The phrases `obsadili jsme Penbrok`, `po příjezdu na Penbrok`, and `okolí Penbroku` concern the castle or its immediate vicinity. Full control of the estate was never established during play. No additional broader physical locality is required.
- Basis: Curator-supplied referent, identity, and play-state clarification during curation review.
- Affected records updated: [campaign record](../campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok.md), [Penbrok castle](../entities/locations/location-penbrok-fortress.md), [Panství Penbrok](../entities/polities/polity-penbrok-estate.md), [Penbrokton](../entities/locations/location-penbrokton.md), and [Střet v Adrianově domě](../events/event-adrian-house-ordeal.md). Existing capture and siege event locations already distinguished the castle and village and required no change.
- Resolution date: 2026-08-21 (real-world archive/curation date).
