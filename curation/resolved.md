## curation-penbrok-019 — Brigitte's origin and affiliation

- Status: superseded
- Scope: [Brigitte](../entities/beings/being-brigitte-shelswell.md) in [Panství Penbrok](../campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok.md)
- Original uncertainty: Where does Brigitte originate, and with which place, household, or other group was she actually affiliated during the campaign?
- Why it matters: The player-note context previously led the archive to associate Brigitte with Sir Adrian's Shelswell household, but the curator clarified that she did not belong in the Shelswell household-fate question. Retaining that association as fact could attach her identity and fate to the wrong context.
- Affected records: [Brigitte](../entities/beings/being-brigitte-shelswell.md), the [player-notes source metadata](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md), and any relationship, location, or event record that might otherwise derive from the occurrence. No other ingested source names Brigitte, supplies an employer or origin, or establishes a played fate. The campaign-version Shelswell GM-preparation document does not name her.
- Earlier handling: Brigitte remained a persistent being because the source explicitly names her. No Shelswell household membership, played fate, alternative origin, or missing beloved identity was asserted pending clarification.
- Withdrawn resolution: During the first answer to this item on 2026-08-22, the curator said that Brigitte had been removed from campaign preparation for simplicity and that mentioning her there was a GM error. The repository briefly derived that her player-note occurrence was not evidence of campaign participation.
- Curator correction: The curator withdrew that answer as erroneous. The curator supplied only a probable reconstruction that she was not killed with Adrian's family, probably escaped, and was not pursued by the player characters because they regarded her as insignificant.
- Supersession: The played encounter is now established, but the original question about Brigitte's origin or affiliation remains unresolved. It is reformulated as [curation-penbrok-020](open.md#curation-penbrok-020--brigittes-affiliation-during-the-played-encounter), which separates the confirmed encounter from the probable fate reconstruction and the still-unknown affiliation.
- Basis: Curator-supplied correction of the previous curation answer, played-encounter interpretation, and uncertain fate reconstruction during curation review on 2026-08-22.
- Affected records updated: [Brigitte](../entities/beings/being-brigitte-shelswell.md), [Střet v Adrianově domě](../events/event-adrian-house-ordeal.md), [player-notes source metadata](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md), and [Shelswell GM-preparation source metadata](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-usedlost-shelswell-gm-preparation.md). ## curation-penbrok-018 — Identity category of æsové

- Status: resolved
- Scope: [Panství Penbrok regional map key](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-penbrok-regional-map-key.md), markers `26` and `50`
- Original uncertainty: What persistent kind of identity, if any, does the expression `z časů æsů` denote: a named people or culture, a polity, an organization, a species or class of beings, an Age, or another historical referent?
- Why it matters: Treating the expression as a polity or organization would manufacture unsupported structure, while ignoring a confirmed proper-named people would leave the named-things invariant incomplete.
- Affected records: [marker-26 aqueduct ruins](../entities/locations/location-penbrok-map-26.md), [castle ruins above Old Chalford](../entities/locations/location-old-chalford-castle-ruins.md), and the [map-key source record](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-penbrok-regional-map-key.md).

- Earlier handling: The exact source expression remained in both affected location records and source metadata. No polity, organization, being, Age, or duplicate identity was asserted until the referent and appropriate persistent type could be established.

They lived there during either the Third Age or the Fourth Age; the curator did not resolve which of the two Ages, so the alternative remains explicit and no exact dates or boundaries are inferred.
- Basis: Curator-supplied polity classification, regional history, capabilities, and uncertain Age during curation review on 2026-08-22.

Yann's and the quest log's broader collective claims remained player-side accounts in the event record; the archive did not reconcile them with the preparation or rank either automatically above the other. Brigitte had already been removed from this household-knowledge question, with her distinct played encounter and unresolved affiliation handled through [curation-penbrok-019](#curation-penbrok-019--brigittes-origin-and-affiliation) and its successor.

In conjunction with their prepared roles, Emma, Jerome, and Tobiáš were the knowing participants; Adrian and Thomas were not. `Everything` is limited here to the worm-slime fish operation under review and does not establish knowledge of unrelated Shelswell secrets.
- Basis: Curator-supplied objective world-state clarification during curation review on 2026-08-22, combined with the prepared operational roles already preserved in the affected records.
