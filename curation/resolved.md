## curation-penbrok-001 — Kristen and Kirsten identity

- Status: resolved
- Scope: [Panství Penbrok](../campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok.md)
- Original uncertainty: Do the spellings `Kristen` and `Kirsten` refer to one being throughout the account?
- Why it matters: A wrong equivalence could merge two beings; a wrong distinction could duplicate one being and fragment her events and relationships.
- Earlier evidence: [Dopisy z panství Penbrok](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok.xml), especially sections `Dobytí Penbroku`, `Upír`, `Dívky a dámy v nesnázích`, `Vrátila se snad válka?`, and `Hořkosladké vítězství`, continues one disappearance-and-return narrative while using both spellings.
- Earlier handling: One being record provisionally held both spellings, with the equivalence explicitly qualified.
- Resolution: `Kristen` and `Kirsten` are variant spellings for the same being in the Panství Penbrok material.
- Basis: Source evidence. The player notes alternate `Kirsten` and `Kristen` within the same immediate scenes in `Sezení 7` and `Sezení 8`, where the woman found at Darkwood remains with the same group and participates in one continuous escape. Together with the correspondence's continuous disappearance-and-return narrative, this removes the earlier identity ambiguity.
- Resolving source and locators: [Player notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md), `Sezení 7` and `Sezení 8`.
- Affected records updated: [Kristen](../entities/beings/person-kristen-penbrok.md). Existing event links already point to that single stable ID and required no retargeting.
- Resolution date: 2026-08-21 (real-world archive/curation date).

## curation-penbrok-006 — Epistemic status of the fortress-curse explanation

- Status: superseded
- Scope: [Penbrok player notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md)
- Original uncertainty: Is the explanation under `Sezení 2` / `Prokletí - ne úplně pravdivá pověst` a GM/meta disclosure, a player hypothesis, a character conclusion, or a post-session explanation available at some other knowledge level?
- Why it matters: Treating it as objective GM information could manufacture certainty about the fortress curse; treating it as Yann's belief could give him knowledge the source does not establish.
- Earlier evidence: [Player notes](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/Penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md), `Sezení 2`, labels the preceding curse story not entirely true and follows it with an explanation, but unlike the explicit `Co postavy neví` line in `Sezení 4`, does not identify the explanation's speaker, source, or knowledge boundary.
- Earlier handling: The explanation was retained as an epistemically ambiguous player/meta note and was not promoted to Yann's knowledge or objective GM truth.
- Supersession: The [GM preparation](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/000-Hrad-Penbrok.docx), key 4 `Panoš a koně`, establishes the matching explanation as hidden prepared baseline. It does not establish when the player learned it or whether Yann knew it. The original question therefore combined a now-resolved content-authority issue with a still-open character-knowledge issue.
- Basis: Source evidence from the pre-play GM preparation.
- Replacement item: [curation-penbrok-008](open.md#curation-penbrok-008--character-knowledge-of-the-curse-explanation).
- Affected records updated: [GM-preparation source metadata](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-hrad-penbrok-gm-preparation.md), [prepared Penbrok snapshot](../snapshots/locations/snapshot-location-penbrok-preplay-baseline.md), and [player-notes source metadata](../sources/campaigns/campaign-dopisy-z-panstvi-penbrok/source-penbrok-hracske-poznamky.md).
