# Skyskarové

## Description

`Skyskarové` is a faction or branch of [giants](being-classification-giant.md) whose members sold themselves to darkness. This classification preserves the source-supported separation from other giants without treating Skyskarové as a distinct species, organization, or polity. It does not define what “darkness” is or add metaphysical mechanics.

- [RPGForum topic 17460, post 690291](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-zelezne-prisahy/rpgforum-340-normalized/topics/17460.md#post-690291) — Havarr describes Skyskarové as storm giants who separated from other giants and chose service to darkness; the account remains attributed where it exceeds the curator-confirmed classification.
