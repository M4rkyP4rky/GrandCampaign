# Zlomení

## Description

`Zlomení` are transformed [humans](being-classification-human.md). They are not an organization, polity, or unrelated species. The cause and mechanism of the transformation remain uncertain: source accounts connect them in different ways with Nemoc and elven magic, but those explanations are not promoted to established causation.

Sources:

- [RPGForum topic 17201, post 650273](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-zelezne-prisahy/rpgforum-340-normalized/topics/17201.md#post-650273) — describes them as people from the Old World who became the group called Zlomení.
- [RPGForum topic 17201, post 687955](../../sources/campaigns/campaign-zelezne-prisahy/rpgforum-340-normalized/topics/17201.md#post-687955) — preserves competing theories about Nemoc, elven magic, and what happened to the Zlomení.
